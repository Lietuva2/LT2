# 8.17 The list of MPs' ballots on a vote

- **One place.** The list lives on the vote's page (`/seimas/balsavimai/{id}`, part `#ballots`), in one component
  (`VoteBallots`, filtered by `BallotList`). A closed bill's page shows the result, the seat map and a link to that part,
  not a copy of the list. A consensus vote and a vote without ballots show no list and no controls.
- **What it shows:** MPs in the order of their last names (Lithuanian alphabet), each with the faction and the
  ballot; the first 20, and "Rodyti daugiau", which adds 50 each time (8.16.4), with "Rodoma {n} iš {m}" beside it.
- **By ballot:** a row of links above the list, "Visi", "Už", "Prieš", "Susilaikė" and the MPs who did not vote, each
  with its count; only the ballots the vote has are offered. The counts are of the ballots listed, so they add up to the
  list even where the official totals differ (the sentence that they do not add up, or another note the Seimas gives, stands beside the result, 7.2).
- **Search:** one field that finds by first name or last name, ignoring case and Lithuanian diacritics
  ("zemaitaitis" finds "Žemaitaitis"); every word typed must be found. Nothing found says so and offers to clear.
- **By faction:** a dropdown beside the search, separate from the text search: "Visos frakcijos" and then the factions
  that have a ballot in this vote, by name, with the MPs without a faction last; a faction label of the per-MP results
  that names no faction body is an option of its own under that label. Built and worded like the one on the MP list
  (`/seimo-nariai`). Search, faction and ballot combine, and the counts beside the ballots are those within the search
  and the faction chosen.
- **"Rodyti daugiau"** grows the list in place (8.16.4).
- **How it works:** the page's own address carries the state (`?q=`, `?frakcija=`, `?balsas=uz|pries|susilaike|nebalsavo`,
  `?rodyti=`). The search and faction use a GET form, and the ballot choices and clear action are links. With enhanced
  navigation, filtering and clearing keep the viewport in place. With JavaScript off, a GET loads the filtered page. Unknown or oversized values (a search over 100 characters) fall
  back to the defaults, never to an error.
- **Surfaces (8.16):** the list with its controls is one sheet.
- Nothing about a signed-in member is on this page, so it stays the same public page for everyone.
