# 8.26 Analytics: where the Seimas and the members agree ("Statistika", `/statistika`)

The public page "Statistika" (`/statistika`) is an item of the main menu (8.21) and has no tab row. It sets the Seimas's
votes beside the members' positions, and shows how united the factions vote. It has no topic filter, nothing about
delegation and nothing that has no data (8.26.7).

## 8.26.1 What the page shows

Public, server-rendered, the same for everyone and nothing about the caller, apart from one island. Two filters in one GET
form on the ground (8.16), with no script, for the whole page: the period ("Laikotarpis", `metai`: the whole current term
by its name, "2024–2028 m. kadencija", the default, or one calendar year of it, Vilnius days) and the accounts counted
("Skaičiuojamos paskyros", `paskyros=senesnes`: "Visos" or "Tik senesnės nei 30 d.", from the counts every record keeps
for them). The closed bills (item 2) have filters of their own in a second GET form above their sheet; they change only
the plot and the table. Each form carries the other's values as hidden fields, so changing one keeps the other. The lead
says once that the numbers are from self-selected Lietuva 2.0 users and are not a representative poll; the established
line ("Tai nėra reprezentatyvi apklausa: …") stands beside the figures and the comparison of sides (8.2, `AGENTS.md`),
with the hint on "maža imtis" beside the figures. The figures say when they were counted ("Suskaičiuota …; skaičiai
atnaujinami kas valandą."): they are a kept copy, at most an hour old (8.26.6).

1. **Four figures.**
   - *Projects members took part in* ("Projektai, dėl kurių pasirinkta pozicija"): of the bills in the catalogue that were
     on the agenda of a sitting held in the period (the catalogue holds only bills discussed at a sitting; registered
     bills are not imported), those whose shown counts have at least one side; under it "Seime svarstyta projektų: N",
     never "registered".
   - *Positions taken* ("Pasirinktos pozicijos", "„už“ ir „prieš“ kartu"): the sum of both sides over the same bills, as
     their pages show them (frozen once closed, live before; a hidden record counts nothing). With the filter, only the
     older accounts' counts.
   - *Majorities agreed* ("Seimo ir naudotojų daugumos pasirinko tą patį"): of the closed bills of the period that are
     compared (8.26.2), those where the members' majority and the Seimas's majority chose the same; "sutapo x iš N
     palygintų projektų, …", with a percentage only when N is 20 or more, otherwise "maža imtis". When N is 0, one line
     says there is no such bill yet.
   - *MPs who took over their profile* ("Seimo nariai, perėmę profilį"): sitting MPs whose profile is taken over (8.5.2),
     of all sitting MPs, today. The period does not apply. Its line is "iš 141 Seimo nario" ("iš 13 Seimo narių": the
     genitive singular after 1, 21, 31 … but not 11, `LithuanianCounts`), never "dabartinių". The number is the MPs of the
     current term whose mandate has not ended: 141 with real data, fewer only while a seat is vacant.
2. **The comparison of sides** ("„Už“ dalis: Seimas ir Lietuva 2.0 naudotojai", a heading that stays in view while its list scrolls, 8.16.5; members' sides are
   positions, never "balsai"). It lists the closed bills of the period with at least one position counted by the
   accounts filter; a closed bill nobody took a side on is not listed.
   - **The plot:** a dot plot drawn by the server with no script, one row per bill shown (see "Its length") that has both
     shares: from 20 positions and a vote with numbers (no consensus, 8.26.2). Each row is about two text lines tall: on
     the left a label column, at most 38 % of the plot's width and 12rem, with the bill's number on the first line and its
     title under it on the second, cut with "…" to the column's width; the whole label is one link to the bill's page,
     with the full "number title" as its tooltip (`title`) and its accessible name. On the same row to the right, the
     bill's track: the Seimas's share "for" as a square and the members' as a dot, with a band between them. Every track
     shares one axis, 0 to 100 % with the line at 50 % and dotted lines at 25 and 75 %, which joins from row to row; the
     axis's words stand under the tracks only, not under the labels (on a phone 0, 50 and 100 % only, so they do not run
     together). Built as an HTML list of rows, each track an SVG laid over its cell; places across are per cent of the
     track, so the plot fits a phone without scrolling. Each mark has a `<title>` with the exact number ("XVP-0412 …:
     Seime už – 62 %"), and the track's name says both. Above it one legend and the line at 50 % ("Linija ties 50 % –
     daugumos riba."); under it one line saying which bills it shows (at least 20 positions, no consensus) and that the
     table has the exact numbers and every bill shown. When none of the bills shown has both shares, one line says so
     instead of the plot.
   - **The table:** "Rodyti lentelę" (`details.more`, open at once when there is no plot) with the same bills as the plot
     and every other bill shown, in the same order: the number with the day of the closing vote, the title linking to the
     bill, the Seimas's share "for" linking to the vote (with the Seimas's own note on the result when it has one;
     "pritarta bendru sutarimu" for a consensus, "rezultato nėra" for a vote without numbers), the members' share "for"
     with the number of positions (under 20 their counts, "2 už, 1 prieš", and "maža imtis"), the difference ("15 %", a
     whole percent like the shares; only when both shares are shown, otherwise "–"), and the majority of those who voted
     in the Seimas ("už", "prieš" or "nėra daugumos", without quotes) with a neutral chip "Daugumos sutapo" or "Daugumos
     skyrėsi" for a compared bill. One hint on "balsavusiųjų dauguma" under the table. On a narrow screen (under 36rem)
     each bill is a block of its own and each figure carries its column's name, so the table never scrolls.
   - **Its filters** (a GET form on the ground above the sheet; a value the page would not make is the default, never an
     error), **closed at first** in a `details.more` whose summary is "Filtrai", with the number of fields set when there
     are any ("Filtrai (2)"; the order counts when it is not the newest first). It is open whenever one of its fields is
     set, so the reader sees what applies and can change it: "Balsuota nuo" / "Balsuota iki" (`nuo`, `iki`: Vilnius days
     of the closing vote, `yyyy-MM-dd` text fields with calendar buttons, both included, within the period; a malformed day is ignored and its
     field says so, keeping what was typed; days the wrong way round are both ignored and a note says so); the search by
     number or title (`q`, every word, ignoring case, as 8.12.5); "Seimo ir naudotojų daugumos" (`rezultatas`: every
     bill, `sutapo`, `skyresi`; only compared bills have either); "Balsavusiųjų dauguma Seime" (`dauguma`: every bill,
     `uz`, `pries`, `nera`, 8.26.2's three); "Rikiuoti" (`rikiuoti`: "Naujausi pirmiau", the default; `skirtumas`, the
     largest difference first; `pozicijos`, the most positions first, as the accounts filter counts them). "Išvalyti"
     clears them and keeps the period. With enhanced navigation, applying or clearing these filters keeps the viewport in place. There is no stage filter: the comparison uses only the closing vote.
   - **Its length:** the first 10 bills of the filtered set in the chosen order are the bills shown, in the plot (those
     with both shares) and in the table (all of them); "Rodyti daugiau" adds 50 (8.16.4) in place by the address
     (`?rodyti=`), up to 300 (the filters narrow a longer list); "Rodoma 10 iš N" says how
     many were found.
   - **The figures do not follow its filters:** the four figures (item 1) and faction unity (item 3) follow the period and
     the accounts counted only, so a search never changes "Majorities agreed". The part's lead says so in one sentence.
   A share that rounds to 50 % without being exactly half is written with as many decimals (up to three) as it takes not
   to read as 50 % ("50,4 %"), so a majority and its absence never look the same.
3. **Faction unity** ("Frakcijų vieningumas"): per faction of the current term with a recorded vote in the period, the
   share of its members' ballots that chose the faction's position, over the votes where it had one (the two-thirds rule
   of 7.3), with a bar, the number of those votes and the number of free votes ("balsavimų su frakcijos pozicija: N ·
   laisvų: M"). Under 20 votes with a position there is no share and no bar, only the counts and "maža imtis"; with
   none, "Balsavimų su frakcijos pozicija nėra." The factions stand in order of their seats today ("narių: N"), then by
   name: side by side, not ranked. A hint says what a faction's position is, with a link to the methodology. Under each a
   `details.more`, "Kur nuomonės skyrėsi labiausiai", lists the three votes where the faction split most (8.26.3), each
   linking to the vote's page.
4. **"Kas Seime balsuoja taip, kaip prašote jūs"**: the member's own comparison (8.26.4), an island filled in the browser.

## 8.26.2 The rules of the comparison (pure functions, `AnalyticsRules` in SharedKernel)

- **A closed bill of the period**: a closed record (8.1; hidden ones are left out) whose closing vote was held in the
  period.
- **The Seimas's share "for"**: for ÷ (for + against + abstain) at the closing vote. A vote adopted by consensus has no
  numbers: it is shown as "pritarta bendru sutarimu", has no dot, and counts as a majority "for". A vote whose per-MP
  results are not read yet is not compared.
- **The Seimas's majority** is the majority of those who voted, one of three: "for" when the votes for are more than half
  of for, against and abstain together (a consensus too); "against" when the votes against are more than half of them;
  otherwise none ("nėra daugumos"). The API carries it as `seimasMajority`: `for` | `against` | `none`. Compared with the
  members, "against" and none both count as not "for": they agree with a members' majority "against" and differ from one
  "for". The proceedings carry the official outcome of a motion in its wording. This page compares ballot majorities,
  labelled "balsavusiųjų dauguma". The vote rows elsewhere show the same majority in
  the same words (8.18, "The majority").
- **The members' share "for"**: for ÷ (for + against) of the counts chosen by the filter. Their majority is the larger
  side; a tie has none and the bill is not compared.
- **The sample**: a bill is compared (a dot, a share, a place in "Majorities agreed") only when the members' counts chosen
  by the filter add up to 20 or more. Under 20 the table gives the counts ("2 už, 1 prieš") with "maža imtis" and no
  share. One number, 20, for every sample on the page (`AnalyticsRules.SmallSample`); it is not a setting.
- **The difference** ("skirtumas"): the distance between the Seimas's share "for" and the members' share "for", only when
  both are shown (not for a consensus, a vote without numbers or under 20 positions); `AnalyticsRules.Difference`.
- **The list's filters and orders** (`AnalyticsRules.MatchesSearch`, `MatchesResult`, `MatchesMajority`, `InDays`,
  `OrderBills`): the result filter keeps only compared bills with that result; the majority filter only bills with that
  majority (a vote without numbers has none); by difference, the bills without one come after all those with one (nothing
  to measure is not "no difference"); every order breaks ties by the newest closing vote, then the number.
- Only a record closed by the adoption-stage vote (as classified, a moderator's correction included, 7.2) is compared. A bill rejected or withdrawn before that stage, whose
  record a moderator closed (8.1 step 6), has no closing vote and is not compared; its members' frozen counts stay on its
  own page.

## 8.26.3 Faction unity (pure functions in Parliament, `FactionUnityRule`)

From the faction positions the import keeps per vote (5.1 `faction_position`), over recorded votes held in the period
(consensus votes have none): the share is the sum, over the votes where the faction had a position, of the members who
chose it, divided by the sum of the members who voted in those votes (pooled, so a large vote weighs more than a small
one). Free votes are counted beside it, never in it. A vote's split is the share of the faction's largest option among
its members who voted; the three lowest are where it split most (free votes included, at least two members voting, the
newest first on a tie). The mixed group is no faction and is not shown. Two labels of the per-MP results that resolve
to the same faction in one vote are one tally: their counts are added and the position worked out again from the sum,
so no vote counts twice. Under 20 votes with a position the share is not given (8.26.1).

## 8.26.4 "Kas Seime balsuoja taip, kaip prašote jūs"

- **For the caller only** (`GET /api/v1/me/analytics/agreement`, members only, rate-limited as the other own reads; a
  visitor gets 401): made from the caller's own sides on closed records and the MPs' public ballots at those records'
  closing votes, the first time the member's page asks for it, and then kept for the member in server memory (8.26.6).
  Never written to a table, the browser or a log, and never in a server-rendered page: the server-side API client
  refuses it, and the page holds only the part's heading and one line ("Tai palyginimas jūsų žiniai, ne Seimo narių
  vertinimas; jį matote tik jūs."). A visitor gets the sign-in link back to the page (`LandingPath.SignInLink`,
  "Prisijunkite ir pamatysite, …") already in the server's HTML, so it works without JavaScript; the page carries
  `<NeedsScript />` for a member without it.
- **An MP matches** on a bill when the MP's ballot at the closing vote is the caller's side; an abstention does not
  match; an MP who did not vote, or a vote by consensus, is not in the sample. **A faction matches** when its position
  (two thirds) is the caller's side; a free vote is not in its sample and is counted beside it ("laisvų balsavimų: N").
  Every MP with a ballot at those votes is listed, with the faction of today (or the last one) and "mandatas baigėsi" for
  an MP whose mandate ended.
- **Order:** factions (with their seats), then MPs; within each, those with 20 common votes or more by the share of
  agreement, highest first, with a bar (then by name); then those with fewer, by name, with "sutapo 3 iš 4 · maža imtis"
  and no bar. A row is the name, the bar and the share on one line; where the list is narrow (a phone) the name takes
  its own line above the bar and the share, so a name never breaks inside a word. MPs by name means as every list of MPs: last name, then first name. Nobody with no common vote is listed.
  The first 10 MPs show; the rest open under "Rodyti visus Seimo narius (N)". The heading line says on how many closed
  bills the caller took a side, and how many of them the Seimas adopted by consensus (no ballots). A member with no side
  on a closed bill gets one line and the link to `/projektai`.
- It is a comparison for the member, not a judgement of MPs: the line under the heading says so and that nobody else sees
  it.

## 8.26.5 What it shows in the trial

The trial has invented data and few members. Faction unity and the take-over count are full. A comparison of sides needs
closed bills with 20 positions: until there are, the plot's place says in one line that none of the bills shown has
both shares, the table (then open) lists them with their counts and "maža imtis", and "Majorities agreed" shows nothing.
The member's own comparison then shows fractions with "maža imtis", or, without a side on any closed bill, one line and
the link to `/projektai`.

**Demonstration data (wherever `Demo:Allowed`: the local stack and the trial):** the stand-in's overlay
(`tools/Lt2.SeimasStandIn/Recent`) adds a sitting nine days ago with an adoption vote, with per-MP ballots, on an
invented bill XVP-0707 "Viešųjų pirkimų įstatymo pataisa"; `--demo-appeal` gives it invented positions taken before the
vote (1 240 for, 1 100 of them older than 30 days; 860 against, 760) and two arguments, and closes its record as the
closing job would. The overlay also holds thirty more invented bills (XVP-0701 to XVP-0731 without XVP-0707), adopted at
thirteen sittings on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 2026-03-24 to 2026-11-03, written by `tools/demo_closed_bills.py` (run
by `tools/standin_overlay.py`) with a fixed seed: per-MP ballots of every MP of the overlay's 141 sitting that day,
groups mostly along their lines with some splits and free votes, some absences, the totals counted from the ballots;
three adopted by consensus, four with no majority "for" of those who voted (one of them, XVP-0706, with more than half
"prieš"). The import reads them as any vote (faction positions included); their sittings are listed under the open
autumn session, because the import reads a closed session's sittings only once. `--demo-appeal` gives each a record with
invented positions taken before its vote from the demonstration's own invented accounts only (those it made for
XVP-0412 and XVP-0707, on `pavyzdys.test`, registered before the vote): from 7 to 2 480 per bill, three under 20, the
members' majority agreeing with the Seimas's on most and not on nine, six bills with arguments; and closes it as
XVP-0707's. So the plot and the table with their filters and "Rodyti daugiau", faction unity, "Majorities agreed" and a
member's comparison can be seen. `--demo-sides-for ADDRESS` (same refusals) gives one existing account that has the
positions consent random sides on these closed bills, dated before their votes and counted in their frozen numbers, so
its owner can see their own comparison; it touches no other account and never logs the address. The example numbers of
XVP-0412 are unchanged. Tests never load the overlay.

## 8.26.6 API and kept copies

```
GET /api/v1/analytics?year=2026&accounts=older     public, with an ETag, nothing about the caller
    &from=2026-03-01&to=2026-10-31&q=…&result=agreed|differed&majority=for|against|none
    &sort=latest|difference|positions&limit=10
GET /api/v1/me/analytics/agreement                 the caller's own comparison; no-store, as every signed-in answer
```

`year` is a calendar year of the current term (anything else is the whole term); `accounts=older` counts only accounts
older than 30 days. The rest is the list's query (8.26.1 item 2): days of the closing vote, the search (at most 100
characters), the result, the Seimas's majority, the order and how many bills to return (1 to 300, 10 by default); a value
the API would not make is its default, and a range that ends before it starts is no days at all. The public answer carries
when it was counted, the four figures, the list's query as applied, how many closed bills of the period with at least one
position there are (`billsClosed`) and how many the filters found (`billsFound`), the first of those in the order with
their numbers (the difference included), and the factions with their three most split votes.

**Kept copies.** Both live in the web process's memory only (`AnalyticsCopies`): never a table, never the browser
(8.22.1), never a cacheable HTTP answer.
- *The public answer*: one copy for everybody per period and accounts counted, holding every closed bill of the period.
  The list's filters, order and length are applied to that copy in memory on each request and make no copy of their own,
  so they never count anything again and cannot multiply the copies (a term holds at most a few hundred closed bills).
  Once it is an hour old it is still served as it is while one refresh runs in the background, so no visitor waits
  except the first after a start or after the data version changed; a copy nobody asked for in a day is let go. Its key
  holds that data version: the closed records (one closes, is hidden or restored) and the last finished import, so those
  changes show at once; sides taken on open bills show within about an hour, and the page says when its numbers were
  counted. Who refreshes is one method (`AnalyticsCopies.Refresh`); no periodic job makes the copy and no table holds it.
- *A member's comparison*: one copy per account, made the first time the member's page asks, kept while the member keeps
  using it (12 hours without a request ends it), made again when the data version changes, and dropped at sign-out
  (also when another account signs in in that browser), account deletion and withdrawal of the positions consent. At most 10 000
  accounts are kept. It reveals the member's sides, so it is treated as a side: never logged, never in an answer to anyone
  else.

## 8.26.7 Not built, and why

- **The topic filter (*sritis*)**: bills carry no subject area, in the data or the design.
- **Party supporters against their faction**: party numbers are not frozen when a record closes, so they would mix today's
  supporters with old sides; a member supports one party, chosen from the nominating parties, not several; and parties do
  not map to factions. It needs its own design (an aggregate made when the record closes, and its own minimum group; whether
  `Digest:PartyGroupMinimum` of the summaries applies is part of that design).
- **"Faction members voted on LT2 as their faction"**: an MP's own account takes no side (8.5.4).
