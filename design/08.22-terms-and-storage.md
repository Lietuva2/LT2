# 8.22 Terms, the privacy notice, and what the browser stores

The terms and the privacy notice are kept correct as drafts as the site changes; the lawyer reads them, with the consent
texts, once before going live.

## 8.22.1 No banner, as a rule of the design

- **Nothing that is not strictly necessary is stored in the browser.** No third-party analytics, no third-party scripts,
  fonts or embeds, no advertising or measurement cookie or storage key. The site counts its own use on its own server,
  in aggregate, from the requests it answers anyway (8.27); that stores and reads nothing in the browser. Consent under
  the e-privacy rules is needed only for storage that is not strictly necessary for the service the person asked for, so
  the site shows no cookie banner. Adding anything that would need one is a design change, not a build detail.
- If such a thing were ever added, its consent would be asked separately and could be refused without losing the
  site; it could not be folded into the terms (consent must be specific and free).
- **The inventory is short and tested.** It is one list in the code, `Security/BrowserStorage.Inventory`, and the
  privacy notice's part "Slapukai ir naršyklės atmintis" is built from it:
  - cookies: the session (`__Host-lt2`), the passkey step (`__Host-lt2.passkey`, up to five minutes), the request-forgery
    cookie (`__Host-lt2.af`, `SameSite=Strict`, until the browser closes) and the known browser (`__Host-lt2.device`,
    8.20.2);
  - local storage: the chosen theme (`lt2-theme`), only once the person chooses one; and the framework's hash of the
    application's files (`blazor-resource-hash:Lt2.Web.Client`), written by Blazor itself whenever an island starts, for
    visitors too, so the next page knows whether the files are loaded; it says nothing about the person;
  - session storage: the sign-in form's state, the page to come back to (`lt2.return-path`) and the invitation code
    (`lt2.invitation-code`); the code is also put there when a link carrying it after the `#` opens any page (8.11.5).
- An end-to-end test (`TermsTests`) visits the site as a visitor and as a member and fails when a cookie or storage key
  outside the notice's list appears, so the notice cannot fall behind the code; an architecture test
  (`BrowserStorageTests`) checks the names against the code that sets them and fails on a script that starts using
  storage. The account item's open state is not stored.

## 8.22.2 Terms of use

- **"Naudojimo taisyklės"** at `/taisykles`: what the account is for, who may register, what may be published, how
  moderation works and how to object (with the link to the moderation rules), that summaries go to MPs, how to leave.
  A draft in the resource files; it carries a version (a date) and "Galioja nuo …", and ends with one line saying it is a
  draft the lawyer will read ("Tai juodraštis: tekstą dar peržiūrės teisininkas.").
- **At registration** the form has one required box, "Sutinku su naudojimo taisyklėmis", with the link, beside the
  confirmation of age, and one plain sentence: "Kaip tvarkome jūsų duomenis, rašoma privatumo pranešime." The accepted
  version is stored as a consent of kind `terms` with its text version (5.2). A pending account accepts with its
  registration and is never asked separately.
- **There is no box for the privacy notice:** it informs, it is not something to agree to. The account rests on the
  terms; only positions rest on consent, which stays its own separate step before the first side (6.5).
- **An account that has not accepted the version in force** sees, after sign-in, one screen,
  `/taisykles/sutikimas` ("Naudojimo taisyklės"), with a short paragraph on what the terms cover, the link, "Sutinku"
  and "Atsijungti", and one line with a link saying that until then the person can read, download their data and delete
  the account; it goes on to the page the person was going to. The screen and the refusals do not say the terms are new
  or have changed; only when terms already published to people really change does the paragraph say what changed. An
  island refused with `terms.required` opens it, and the account page shows a note with the link.
- Until then the account reads everything and its writes are refused with `terms.required`, except accepting, signing
  out and deleting the account, and withdrawing what it consented to: switching the public profile off
  (`DELETE /me/profile/public`, which changes nothing else; the form's `PUT /me/profile` stays refused), withdrawing the
  positions consent (`DELETE /me/consents/positions`), the party support consent (`DELETE /me/party-support`) and the
  party shown on the public profile (`DELETE /me/party-support/public`). The export is a read and is never refused. The
  MP's mail links (8.19.4) and a member's stop link (8.24.1) are exempt as well: they act for whoever holds the link's
  token, not for the account signed in, and people without an account use them. `TermsExemptions.Routes` lists exactly
  these; giving a consent again stays refused.
- **The version.** The terms are at version 2026-11-17. While nothing is launched, nobody has accepted an earlier text,
  so a change to the text keeps the version and nobody is asked again. From the launch, a changed text gets a later
  version, and every member is asked again.

## 8.22.3 The privacy notice

- **"Privatumo pranešimas"** at `/privatumas`: who is responsible, what is kept and why (the address, sides as
  special-category data under consent, what a person publishes, follows, sessions and known browsers, hashed addresses
  in logs for 30 days, and "Lankomumo skaičiai": address and browser, or the account, hashed with a salt of the day,
  month and year; while the period lasts the salt and the summary are kept and could show whether a given address or
  account was counted, the deleted account's too; then only counts; administrators see them), who else processes it,
  how long, the person's rights and how to use them on the account page (export, deletion, withdrawing consent), where
  to complain. Its part on the person's rights says that the link "atsisakyti laiškų" at the bottom of every email about
  what they follow turns all of those emails off, without signing in. A draft, versioned like the terms (2026-11-17),
  ending with the same draft line.
- Its part **"Slapukai ir naršyklės atmintis"** lists the inventory of 8.22.1 with what each is for and how long it
  lasts, and says there are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies of any kind and that the site counts its use
  on its server.
- The facts the design does not give are marked "[bus įrašyta]" in the text: who is responsible and their contacts, the
  provider's details in the terms, the email, server and backup providers, the inspectorate's contacts, where other
  requests go, and the legal basis of the technical logs and of the usage statistics.
- Linked from the footer of every page (without script), the registration form, the consent before the first side, and
  every email's footer.

## 8.22.5 Link previews

- **Meta tags only.** A public, server-rendered page carries in its head the Open Graph tags (`og:site_name` "Lietuva 2.0",
  `og:locale` `lt_LT`, `og:type` (`article` for a bill, `website` otherwise), `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:url`,
  `og:image` with its type, width, height and alt text), `twitter:card` `summary_large_image`, and the plain
  `<meta name="description">`. No script, pixel, SDK or button of any network: nothing is added to the browser (8.22.1),
  and the networks read the tags when someone shares a link.
- **The address is the page's own** (`og:url`): absolute from the site's public address (`Site:Address`, 5.6), never from
  the request's host, and only the path: no query, no fragment, so no code or token (6.8) and no page state. The image is
  absolute the same way. Without a configured address (a development machine) both are left out.
- **One image for every page but the front page, a bill's and a vote's page:** a static 1200×630 PNG with the logo and "Lietuva 2.0"
  on the brand's colours (`wwwroot/img/link-preview.png`, made once from the real logo and font). The front page has its
  own (owner, 2026-11-26): the small logo with "Lietuva 2.0", the slogan in two lines (the second half in the brand's
  green) and its subline, centred (`wwwroot/img/link-preview-home.png`). Each address carries the file's fingerprint, so
  a changed image is a new address. Other sites may fetch it (`Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy: cross-origin` for `/img`).
  The image keeps its content (the logo above, the name centred below it) inside the centred square, because some apps
  (Facebook on phones) crop it so; only the band along the bottom reaches the edges.
- **A vote's image is its seat map** (owner, 2026-11-26): the small logo with "Lietuva 2.0", the line "Seimo balsavimas ·
  {stage} · {yyyy-MM-dd}", the title the page's preview names (at most two lines, cut with "…"), the seat map coloured by
  each MP's choice as on the page (7.3; no names) and its legend with the numbers, inside the centred square, on the same
  ground and band; when the label line does not fit, it is smaller and then drops "Seimo balsavimas". It is drawn on the
  server when first asked for, never ahead, and kept in memory (bounded) for the next
  one; its address (`/img/votes/{id}/{version}.png`) carries a digest of what is drawn, so a changed title or ballot is a
  new address, the answer may be kept for a year, and an older version's address leads to the current one. The fonts are
  the site's own (Sofia Sans, OFL), carried in the application. A vote without per-MP ballots (a consensus, no results
  yet) has the site's image.
- **A bill's image is its title card** (owner, 2026-11-27): the small logo with "Lietuva 2.0", the line "Seimo projektas ·
  XVP-0412", the title the bill's preview names (at most four lines, cut with "…"), the stage line as the page's header has
  it (the planned stage and "Planuojama {yyyy-MM-dd}", else the latest stage the Seimas voted on; "Uždarytas" for a closed
  record) and, only while the record is open, "Paprašykite Seimo narių balsuoti už ar prieš" in the brand's green; inside
  the centred square, on the same ground and band. Never the counts (below). It is drawn, kept and addressed like a vote's
  (`/img/bills/{number}/{version}.png`).
- **Both drawn images are sized for a phone** (owner, 2026-11-27), where a feed shows them at about a third: titles 56–64 px
  in balanced lines, no text under 28 px.
- **What each page says.** The text is one line of at most about 200 characters, whole sentences where they fit.
  - A bill: "XVP-0412 · {title}" (the readable title when there is one, whoever wrote it, 8.13.1) and the description's first sentences, an AI
    one included; while a report about the description is open, neither the readable title nor the description (8.13.1,
    "Off the site"). Otherwise the title and "Seimo projektas.
    Lietuva 2.0 naudotojai prašo balsuoti už ar prieš ir pateikia argumentus." (after the final vote "prašė … pateikė";
    a finished bill nobody took part in or a hidden record: "Seimo projektas: stadijos ir balsavimai Seime.").
    **Never the counts:** numbers need "not a representative poll" beside them, which a preview cannot keep.
  - An MP: the name; "Seimo narys" (or the profile's own label) and the faction after a colon, then what the page holds.
  - An organisation: its name and, while active, its own description; a blocked organisation has no preview.
  - A member's public page: the name and one generic line, never "Apie mane" or anything they wrote; a retired or unknown
    profile has none.
  - A vote: "Balsavimas · {title}" (the title as the bill's preview has it, else the vote's heading); the text "Seimo
    balsavimas {yyyy-MM-dd}: už {n}, prieš {n}, susilaikė {n}." (the Seimas's own numbers, not participants') or "Seimo
    balsavimas {yyyy-MM-dd}: pritarta bendru sutarimu.", then the bill's description as the bill's preview has it (none
    while a report is open). No majority's word ("Dauguma už"): what it means is one click away on the page, which a
    preview cannot keep. A vote without results has the site's lead sentence.
  - A summary's public copy, the agenda, "Projektai", "Statistika", "Metodika": the page's title and one sentence of what
    it is; the front page: the slogan, "Padėkite Seimui jus atstovauti.", and "Seimo projektai, balsavimai ir Seimo
    nariai vienoje vietoje. Pasakykite Seimui, ko prašote, ir pamatykite, ką jis nusprendė." The site's lead sentence
    ("Matykite, ką Seimas svarstys artimiausiomis dienomis, ir pasakykite Seimo nariams, ko prašote.") is the
    description of every other public page that gives none of its own (the MPs' and organisations' lists, the
    legal texts …).
- **No preview and never indexed:** the account screens, staff screens, signing in and accepting the terms, an MP's own
  pages and the pages a one-time link opens (take-over, office and representative invitations), and registering an
  organisation (`X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, `SecurityHeaders.IsPrivatePage`); the token pages of 8.19.4 and
  8.24.1 are `noindex` and `no-store` too.
