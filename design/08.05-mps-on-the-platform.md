# 8.5 MPs on the platform

An MP's account is recognised by its address, takes the MP's profile over with a passkey, may add an office, and
answers on bills once for everyone. All of 8.5.1–8.5.7 is built.

## 8.5.1 Recognised at once

- **The official address** of an MP is the email contact from open data on the Seimas's own domain (`lrs.lt`, not a
  sub-domain), compared ignoring case. Only an MP whose mandate has not ended has one for this purpose, and an address
  listed for more than one such MP belongs to nobody. The Parliament module answers "whose official address is this"
  through an interface of the shared kernel; no address leaves it otherwise.
- **An account whose proven address is an MP's official address is told so at once:** right after registration is
  completed, and on the account's pages every time until the profile is taken over: that this is the official address of
  that MP, by name, with one button, "Perimti profilį". (Every text that carries an MP's name is worded so the name
  stands in the nominative, after a colon or on its own: names are not declined by the software.) Nothing is linked and
  nothing is public before the take-over is finished: registering does not by itself put a mark on anyone's profile.
- **Shown on "Profilis" and on every page.** "Profilis" (`/paskyra`, 8.21) has one line about it: before the take-over
  "Seimo narys: {name}." with "Perimti profilį", a link to the offer on "Dalyvavimas" (`/paskyra/dalyvavimas#mp-offer`);
  after it, "Tvarkote Seimo nario profilį: {name}." (an office member: "Esate Seimo nario biuro narys. Seimo narys:
  {name}.") with "Profilis" and "Tvarkyti" (the MP part on "Dalyvavimas", headed "Seimo nario profilis"). And every page
  carries, under the header, the notice "Prisijungėte oficialiu Seimo nario adresu. Seimo narys: {name}." with "Perimti
  profilį" to the same place, while the offer stands; not on the take-over page and not on "Dalyvavimas", which holds
  the offer itself. Both come only from the account's own `GET /me` in the browser: the served page is the same for
  every member (the notice's markup is there, hidden, without a name), and a member's page reads `GET /me` once per page
  load for it (kept in the page's memory, nothing stored in the browser).
- **Registration without an invitation.** While registration needs an invitation, an official address may register
  without one: the mailbox is the proof. It is a setting, `Registration:OfficialAddressesWithoutInvitation`, **off by
  default**: without an invitation anyone can make the site send a sign-in code to an MP's mailbox (within the limits
  of 6.2), so on a real server it is switched on only together with the introduction letter (8.19.5). The local stack
  and the trial site switch it on, where the MPs are invented and every email is caught.

## 8.5.2 Taking over the profile

A sign-in code has six digits and can be guessed at a known address: about 2 % a year for one address under constant
attack (6.2), which across 141 public addresses is a few accounts a year. That is accepted for a member's own account;
it is not acceptable for publishing in an MP's name. So recognition is at once, but the take-over itself is finished
through a link that cannot be guessed, and it binds a passkey (6.2).

1. "Perimti profilį" (`POST /api/v1/mps/{slug}/claim`) sends a confirmation link to the official address, which is the
   account's own address: good for 24 hours and for one use, stored hashed; a new one replaces the old; at most three a
   day per MP. The secret is in the link's fragment, so it reaches no log.
2. The link opens `/seimo-narys/perimti` for the signed-in account (it asks to sign in first otherwise), which says what
   taking over means: the profile will show that the MP or the office manages it; this account will speak for the MP
   and will no longer take sides itself (8.5.4); a passkey is made now and will be the account's only one.
3. "Sukurti prieigos raktą ir perimti" makes the passkey in the browser and, in one request
   (`POST /api/v1/me/mp/takeover`), checks the token and that the account's address is still that MP's official
   address, stores the passkey, removes the account's other passkeys and their sessions, links the account to the
   person, and withdraws the account's own sides on bills whose record is not closed (a closed record's counts are
   frozen, 6.9, and keep its sides). The session becomes a passkey session under a new session key. It is audited and
   the official address gets a notice.
4. **A lost passkey** is replaced the same way ("Pametėte prieigos raktą? Siųsti naują nuorodą"): a new link, a new
   passkey, the old ones removed. No administrator is needed, and a sign-in code alone never changes an MP account's
   passkeys: on a linked account adding or removing a passkey needs a passkey session or the link.
5. **The link's secret can be typed.** It is the typeable one-time code of 6.8 (24 hours, one use, a new one replaces
   the old): the email gives "Perimti profilį" as a link, then the page `/seimo-narys/perimti` and the code to type
   there; opened without the link (signed in), the page asks for "Kodas" and goes on to the passkey step as from the
   link.

- **The link is to the person,** by the Seimas's person number, so it survives a change of the official address. One
  account per MP and one MP per account. When the mandate ends the link stays and the account can read, but nothing
  new can be published (8.5.5).
- **Ending it.** The MP's account can give the profile up ("Atsisakyti profilio", `DELETE /api/v1/me/mp`); an
  administrator can end a link with a fresh passkey confirmation and a statement (misuse, a wrong link) through
  `POST /api/v1/admin/mps/{slug}/link/end`. Both are audited and told to the MP's account's own address. The
  administrator's screen is "Paskyros" (`/administravimas/paskyros`, the account item, 8.21), first section "Seimo narių
  profiliai": the MPs whose profile is taken over now (`GET /api/v1/admin/mps/links`: name, profile link and the day it was
  taken over, oldest first; never the account, its address or its office), each with "Baigti ryšį", which opens the
  statement field (required) in place under that MP, one at a time like every action's fields (8.3), with a sentence on what
  it does (the account stops managing the profile, the office's access ends with it, published responses stay, the account's
  owner is emailed the statement); the press asks for the staff passkey when the last confirmation is more than ten minutes
  old, on the same press, and sends again with the statement kept (6.2). Afterwards the list is read again and says "Ryšys
  baigtas. Seimo narys: {vardas}." Anyone who is not an administrator in a staff-passkey session is told the page is for
  administrators and sees none of it. The responses stay as published (8.5.5). Deleting the account ends the link the same way. Ending the link
  ends the office with it.
- **What the public sees:** on the MP's profile one line, "Profilį tvarko Seimo narys arba jo biuras", with the date. A
  profile nobody took over says nothing about joining, because that would read as a reproach, and there is never a
  count of unanswered bills.

## 8.5.3 The office

- The MP's account can add up to five **office members**, who write on the MP's behalf. It makes a one-time invitation
  (stored hashed, 7 days, one use) and either has the site email it or takes the link and code and passes them on by
  its own means; a member who accepts in a passkey session becomes an office member of that MP. An account is an office
  member of at most one MP, and the MP's own account of none.
- The MP's account sees its office members by name and can remove any; an office member can leave ("Palikti biurą").
  All of it is audited and told to the MP's account's own address, never naming the member. Who the office members are
  is never public.
- An office member is otherwise an ordinary member with their own account, sides and follows. On their account, too,
  a passkey can be added or removed only in a passkey session once they are an office member; an office member who
  lost it is removed and added again.
- **Every text is marked the same,** "Seimo narys arba jo biuras": a shared mailbox cannot tell who typed, and the
  public is not told which account did.
- **Everything on the invitation's one page.** The page `/seimo-narys/biuras#…` does it all, the token staying after the
  `#` throughout (never in a query, a log or the browser's storage):
  1. **Not signed in** (or a registration not finished): the sign-in and registration steps are on the page itself (the
     code by email, then the name, the age and the terms of use), and each step reloads this same page, token and all.
  2. **Signed in by code, no passkey on the account:** one button, "Pridėti prieigos raktą ir priimti". It makes a passkey
     in the browser and sends it with the token in one request (`POST /api/v1/me/mp/office/acceptance`), which stores it
     and accepts in one transaction.
  3. **Signed in by code, with a passkey:** "Prisijungti prieigos raktu ir priimti" (an assertion with one of the
     account's passkeys, in the same request), and beside it "Šiame įrenginyje rakto nėra? Pridėti prieigos raktą ir
     priimti" for a device without it.
  4. **A passkey session:** "Priimti".

  After 2 or 3 the session becomes a passkey session under a new session key (the code session's row is deleted, as when
  a code session becomes a staff session, 6.2). The rules hold throughout: one office per person and none for the MP's
  own account, five per MP, the invitation working only while its maker is the MP's account, audited, the MP's account
  told, nobody named publicly. **Why a passkey may be added here by code:** the rule that a linked account changes its
  passkeys only in a passkey session (8.5.2) is about linked accounts, and the invitee is not linked until the request
  succeeds; any member may add a passkey in a code session from the account page, then sign in with it and accept, so
  the one request allows nothing those steps did not. The checks come first: a wrong or used token, or an account
  already linked (the MP's own account by code included), is refused before anything is stored, so a linked account
  never gets a passkey by code; another account's passkey proves nothing; an account with an active staff role adds a
  passkey only in its staff session (6.2), as everywhere. A passkey added this way is told to the account like any other.
- **The site sends the invitation; it counts as an invitation; and it can be typed.**
  - On "Dalyvavimas" the MP's account (in a passkey session) types the office member's address and presses "Siųsti
    kvietimą" (`POST /api/v1/me/mp/office/invitations` with `email`): the site emails the invitation ("Kvietimas į Seimo
    nario biurą Lietuva 2.0": who invites, what an office member does, "Priimti kvietimą" as a link, the page and the
    code to type instead, until when it works). The address is used only to send it: it is in the outbox row until the
    email goes and nowhere else, not on the invitation and not in the audit (which says only that it was emailed). "Gauti
    nuorodą ir kodą" gives the link (shown once) and the code for the MP's account to pass on itself.
  - The invitation's secret is the typeable one-time code of 6.8; the page opened without it asks for "Kvietimo kodas".
  - A person without an account registers on the page itself with no invitation code: the office invitation counts as
    the closed trial's invitation (6.2).
  - Limits: five members per MP, seven days, one use, and, against a runaway sender, at most ten invitations a day per
    MP, copied or emailed (`PoliticianRules.OfficeInvitationsPerDay`; `mp.office-invitation-limit`).
- **An office member gets copies of the MP's summaries,** by their own choice of how often, whatever the MP chose;
  leaving the office ends them (8.19.3).

## 8.5.4 What a linked account can do

- **Speaking for the MP** (publishing, changing and withdrawing a response; adding and removing office members; giving
  the profile up or leaving the office; the MP's choice of summaries) needs a **passkey session**. Signed in by a code,
  the account can read everything of its own, and the controls are off; in their place "Šiam veiksmui reikia
  prisijungimo prieigos raktu." with "Patvirtinti prieigos raktu" makes the session a passkey session in place (6.2).
- **The MP's own account takes no part as a participant:** no side, no argument, no "Man svarbu", no party-support
  declaration, and it does not represent an organisation, and it comments nowhere but under its own MP's response, as
  the MP (8.23.2). The MP speaks through the response. Sides the account held are withdrawn at the take-over (the page
  says so beforehand) and the counts go down. It can follow bills and MPs like anyone. Office members are not
  restricted.
- **The forms are not shown to it.** The server's refusal (`mp.takes-no-part`) stays as the safety net, but the MP's own
  account is not offered what it would refuse: the side buttons on the bill's page (one line in their place, "Seimo
  nario paskyra pozicijos neužima." with a link to the responses' part) and in the front page's rows (the numbers stay),
  the positions consent, writing an argument, "Man svarbu" (the number stays, as text), "Sutinku" and the comment box
  except under its own MP's response, the party and "Rodyti viešame profilyje" (a party given before the take-over stays
  there to remove), "Viešas profilis", and registering an organisation (on `/organizacijos/nauja` one line, "Seimo nario
  paskyra organizacijų neregistruoja."). The islands learn it in the browser only, from the account's own answers
  (`GET /me`'s `mp.part`, read once per page and shared; `GET /me/bills`'s `mpAccount` for the front page): the page the
  server renders is the same as for any member, and the forms go once the island runs. An office member sees every form
  and acts in their own name; under their MP's response the comment voice stays the MP's.
- It is not a role in the session: every request that speaks for an MP reads the link from the database, as the
  staff checks do, so ending a link works at once.
- The account's own answer (`GET /me`) says whether there is an offer to take over, and for a linked account which
  MP, in which part (the MP or the office) and whether the session may write. Nothing about it is in any public
  answer or in the page the server renders.

## 8.5.5 The response

- **One per MP per bill:** a stance, "balsuosiu už", "balsuosiu prieš" or "dar neapsisprendžiau", and an explanation
  as long as the members' texts setting allows (`Texts:MaxLength`, 2 000 by default, 5.6), shown whole up to about
  1 500 characters and longer behind "Rodyti daugiau" (8.2). It is written on the bill's page by the MP's account or an
  office member in a passkey session and is public at once, marked "Seimo narys arba jo biuras".
- **Until the vote.** It can be written, changed and withdrawn while the bill is open (until the adoption vote, as a
  side can be taken); then it is frozen. An MP whose mandate has ended publishes nothing new. An account under a
  moderator's writing restriction (8.3) cannot publish a response, as it cannot publish a statement.
- **Its history is public,** because it is a public office holder's public answer: a changed response says "Keista"
  with the date and keeps its earlier versions behind "Ankstesnės versijos"; a withdrawn one stays as "Atsakymas
  atsiimtas" with the date and its versions; written again it is the same response. Nothing is silently replaced. Each
  version shows what it changed against the one before it, word by word, with the text before the change whole under
  "Rodyti tekstą prieš pakeitimą"; the oldest is shown whole, a published response's own text opens the list with its
  changes, and a withdrawn one's withdrawal ("atsakymas atsiimtas {data}", the text struck through and whole under the
  disclosure) (8.16.2).
- **After the vote** the response stands beside how the MP voted on the adoption ("Atsakė: balsuos už. Balsavo:
  prieš."), two facts side by side without a word of judgement. They are never added up: no share of "kept" answers,
  no ranking, on any page (7.1's rule against scorecards).
- **Moderation after the fact,** as for an organisation's statement (8.14.9): the same screening puts a flagged
  response in the moderators' queue, and reports (target `response`) work as for statements. A moderator can leave it
  ("Palikti", `keep`), hide one that breaks the rules, with a statement that is shown in its place ("Atsakymas
  paslėptas moderatorių." and the reason), and restore it while the bill is open. A hidden response's text and versions
  are not shown. The hiding and the restoring are emailed to the MP's account's own address (the official address when
  the profile was taken over), as the notices of 8.5.2 and 8.5.3 are, because no address leaves the Parliament module
  (8.5.1); with no account linked as that MP no email goes, and the statement stays public in the response's place.
- **Not counted among participants.** A response is not a side: it changes no count, no threshold and no summary's
  numbers.
- **Privacy and deletion.** No public answer says which account wrote a response, and the table
  (`appeals.mp_response`) is withheld from the read-only role. Deleting an account that wrote responses leaves them, as
  the MP's public answers, without the account's number; the account's export lists the responses it wrote.

## 8.5.6 Where responses show

- **The bill's page:** a part "Seimo narių atsakymai", the first of the texts that run at the page's full width below the
  bill's top part, before the organisations' statements and the arguments, server-rendered and the same for everyone:
  one line saying how many MPs answered and how they split (published responses only), then the responses in the order
  "už", "prieš", "neapsisprendė", within each by last name, then the withdrawn and hidden ones; each with the MP's name
  linking to the profile, the faction, the mark, the text and the dates. The first 10, then "Rodyti daugiau atsakymų"
  adds 20 by the page's address (`?atsakymai=`; 8.16.4). No response: no part; on an open
  bill the linked account's form (below) then brings the part's sheet and heading with it, for that account only. A
  bill whose record a moderator hid shows no part (the page shows the bill only, 8.12.3).
- **The MP's profile:** a part "Atsakymai" after the votes: the bills answered, newest first, each with the stance,
  the text and, after the vote, the MP's ballot; the first 10 and "Rodyti daugiau atsakymų", 20 more a press (8.16.4).
  No responses: no part.
- **For the linked account,** on the bill's page, a small island with the form (stance, text; "Skelbti atsakymą",
  "Keisti", "Atsiimti"), filled in the browser, so the page's HTML stays the same for everyone.
- **"Aptarimas"**, members' comments under each response on the bill's page, with the MP's own comments under its
  response marked "Seimo narys arba jo biuras", while `Comments:OnResponses` is on (the default): 8.23. Not on the
  profile.

## 8.5.7 "Projektai, dėl kurių į jus kreipiamasi"

A page for a linked account (the MP or an office member), `/seimo-narys/projektai`, reached from the MP part on
"Dalyvavimas" (`GET /api/v1/me/mp/bills`; a code session may read it): the bills that are in summaries now (they reached
the threshold and are open), in the summary's order, each with its number, title, stage, "Planuojama …", both sides'
numbers (one each, as every list of bills: owner, 2026-11-21; the summary email carries the counts for accounts older
than 30 days and the "not a representative poll" note), and the MP's response or a link to answer. It is the summary
email's list on a page, live; nothing stands under the list. Its sheet has no visible heading, so the page's title stays
at the top of the screen while the list scrolls past (8.16.5). Nothing about other MPs is on it.

After the summaries' bills the page lists every other bill this MP has a response on, in any state the MP's own view
shows (published, withdrawn, hidden by the moderators), newest change first, once each: its number, title and
"Planuojama …" while it is open, "Dabar suvestinėje nėra" in place of the numbers, and the response, with "Atsakyti"
only while the bill is open. A first response follows the bill for the account that wrote it (the MP's or an office
member's), as a first side does (8.11.2): the response is saved first and is never refused or undone because the follow
failed; editing it, withdrawing it or writing it again after a withdrawal follows nothing, and "Nebesekti" keeps the
response. So an answered bill is also in the account's "Jūsų projektai" and follow list.

## 8.5.8 Done when

The tests of 8.5.1–8.5.7 (`MpLinkTests`, `MpResponseTests`) hold these: an account registered with an MP's official
address is told so at once and, with the setting on, needs no invitation; "Perimti profilį" sends the link only to that
address; the link with a new passkey links the account, removes its other passkeys, withdraws its sides and shows the
mark on the profile; a guessed or reused token, another account's token and an address that is no longer official are
refused; a lost passkey is replaced by a new link; an office member is added by an invitation, writes in a passkey
session and can be removed; signed in by a code a linked account cannot publish; the MP's own account cannot take a
side; a response is public at once with its mark, keeps its versions when changed and stays as withdrawn when withdrawn;
after the adoption vote it is frozen and shows the ballot beside it; a moderator can hide and restore it with a public
statement; no page, answer or log says which account wrote a response or who an MP's office members are; the profile of
an MP who has not taken it over says nothing about joining; the pages work at 390 px, and the public parts without
JavaScript.
