# 8.7 Public record of what was sent

Every summary has a public page (`/suvestines/2026-11-16`) with the frozen numbers and reasons and, under each bill, the
organisations it named (8.14.4: the same sentences as the email, each name a link to its statement on the bill's page),
without MP-specific figures and without the reasons' authors (8.4). Participants can see that their appeal reached the Seimas, journalists
can cite it, and MPs know that the numbers in their inbox are the numbers everyone sees. The pages carry the same "not a
representative poll" notice.

**From the bill.** The bill's page lists the summaries that held it, newest first: the day, the kind, and both sides as
frozen there with the counts for accounts older than 30 days, each linking to that bill's item on the summary's public
page. Before the first it says the bill has not been in a summary yet. The summaries keep their own menu item and pages;
each item there links back to the bill's page. The link is `/suvestines/{day}?projektas={number}`: the page opens at its
top, the day's summaries that hold the bill come first and the bill's item is the first under each one's heading, so
nothing is scrolled. The items keep their ids for other links. A day with one summary has one title, its kind and date
("Savaitinė suvestinė · 2026 m. lapkričio 16 d."); a day with several has the day's title and a heading for each.

**The front page.** The front page (`/`, `Home.razor`) is public and server-rendered: the agenda with what a person can
do about it. The mockup is kept under `design/ui/`.

- **A visitor** sees, in this order: the head; "Kaip tai veikia", four numbered steps (the Seimas plans; you say what you
  ask; you explain why; MPs learn of it, with "Kol vyksta bandymas, suvestinės sudaromos, bet Seimo nariams
  nesiunčiamos." while sending is off, 8.19.1), and the link "Kas matoma viešai, o kas ne →" to the methodology page;
  then the agenda. The same steps stand at the top of `/metodika` (`#how-it-works`). This introduction is the one
  place where the short-texts rule of 8.15 does not apply: a new person must learn what the site is. There is no
  sign-in form on the page: the form stays on `/prisijungti`.
  - **The head.** The page's heading is the slogan, "Padėkite Seimui jus atstovauti." (owner, 2026-11-26), at the h2
    step (`--fs-5`), its second half in the brand's green, and under it the subline as a lead paragraph: "Pasakykite
    Seimui, ko prašote, ir pamatykite, ką jis nusprendė."; the page's title is "Lietuva 2.0". Under it the trial note, "Vyksta uždaras bandymas: registruotis galima
    tik su kvietimo kodu.", and its links: "Turite kvietimo kodą? Registruokitės · Jau turite paskyrą? Prisijunkite". From
    54rem the headline and the note are one column a line of text wide, so the note shares both edges of the
    invented-data notice; the turning logo (the page's only one), about 15rem wide, stands centred in the free space to
    the right, vertically in the whole space between the menu and the rule above "Kaip tai veikia", the invented-data
    notice included: on this page the notice is the head's first row (the layout leaves it out there), and where there
    is no notice the logo centres on the head alone. On a phone the small logo stands beside the headline (set smaller,
    `--fs-4`, the subline at body size) and the note goes under both. The agenda's heading shows on the first screen at 1280 × 900.
  - **Following** is not explained in the introduction. What following is and sends is said on the methodology page in
    "Kas matoma viešai, o kas ne", in its own paragraph (`/metodika#following`), beside the sentence that only the person
    sees what they follow, and in the hint beside every "Sekti" button (8.11.2).
- **The agenda** is two lists, each a timeline on a sheet of its own, with the page's ground between them (owner,
  2026-11-21: the planned days run forward and the voted ones back, so each has its heading). Each list's heading row
  holds its heading (both h2) and, beside it, the link to the list's long form, and stays at the top of the screen while
  its sheet scrolls past (8.16.5). One block per day: "Planuojama" or "Balsuota" with the date and the weekday, and how
  many the day holds.
  - **"Artimiausiu metu Seime"**, with "Visa darbotvarkė →" beside the heading and the line on how often the agenda
    changes and when it was last read: the next two sitting days, five bills of each in the agenda's order (8.11.1,
    "Days and their length": activity, then stage, then number).
  - **"Neseniai balsuota"**, with "Ankstesni balsavimai →" beside the heading, leading to the history of votes (8.18):
    the last two voting days of the last seven days, five votes of each (`AgendaRules.FrontDays`, `FrontPerDay`), or
    under the heading the line that the Seimas did not vote on a bill's stage in those days; the list shows either way.
  - **Nothing stands under the lists:** no note on the numbers, no hint and no "not a representative poll" notice. The
    bill's page, the summaries, the statistics page and the methodology say what the numbers are (owner, 2026-11-21).

  Under a longer day stands the shared line "Rodoma 5 iš 40 · Rodyti daugiau", as on the agenda page (8.11.1, 8.16.4):
  each press adds 50 rows of that day in place by the address, `/?diena=2026-11-24:55` (a
  voted day `?balsuota=2026-11-17:55`); without script the server renders the
  page with the day grown, with script the site's enhanced navigation swaps it in without a reload; the rows not shown
  are not in the page. A bill the open data does not know yet is counted in its day and listed (folded into one line)
  after the day's last known row, once every known row shows. Without any planned item the page says so. The page holds
  at most twenty rows until a day grows.
  - Each coming bill shows its number, stage and title (the title is the way to the bill's page); a date a moderator
    set carries "datą patikslino moderatorius"; both sides, one number each ("už 6 800 · prieš 4 280", no count in
    brackets: the bill's page carries the counts for accounts older than 30 days), the bar and the threshold; the
    organisations that have a public statement on it, as names with their stance, each linking to its profile (an
    unconfirmed one keeps the short tag "Nepatvirtinta" after its name, so nobody takes an unconfirmed name for a
    checked one; the full label is on the bill's page and the profile); and, on an open record, the two
    sides to take (below). Beside the side buttons no line says that nobody took part yet; a row without buttons (a
    voted bill's) says "Niekas nenurodė, ko prašo". A hidden record says "Dalyvavimas uždarytas" and a closed one shows
    its numbers ("Niekas nedalyvavo" when nobody did), each with "Į projekto puslapį →". An item that is not in the open
    data yet shows what the agenda says and a link to the source ("Darbotvarkėje lrs.lt"). The agenda page's rows are
    these rows, one component (`TimelineItem`, `VotedItem`; 8.11.1).
  - A voted bill shows the result with the majority of those who voted (8.18, "The majority"), the bill's numbers and
    "Kaip balsavo Seimo nariai", leading to the vote's page.
- **Taking a side in the row.** Under a coming bill's numbers the row says "Prašau balsuoti" and offers two buttons, "už"
  and "prieš", so a person with a position can take it at once; the title is the way to the details.
  - A member presses one and the side is saved at once: the same request and the same rules as on the bill's page
    (8.12.1: the consent, one position per person per bill, the limits). The pressed button shows their side (a check
    mark); pressing the other one changes it; pressing the pressed one again withdraws it, with the effects of a
    withdrawal (the counts, the threshold, the summaries; "Man svarbu" marks stay and stop counting, 8.2). The pressed
    button says it is pressed (`aria-pressed`), and its title says "Paspaudę dar kartą, atsiimsite."; no line under the
    buttons explains it. Against an accidental double press: the buttons are disabled while a request runs, the second
    click of a double click (`MouseEvent.detail` above 1) does nothing, and the withdrawal names the side being
    withdrawn, so the server changes nothing when the person no longer has it. A closed bill changes nothing.
  - The first side a member ever takes needs the positions consent (6.5). It is asked right in the row, across the
    row's width, with the same text, checkbox and buttons as on the bill's page (one shared component,
    `PositionConsentForm`); nothing is saved before it is given, and "Atšaukti" leaves the row as it was.
  - After the member's own action the row shows that bill's live numbers, read from the bill's public answer, not the
    front page's copy that may be a minute old: a person who has just pressed "už" sees the count they are part of. The
    other rows stay as they were.
  - A visitor's two buttons are links to sign in that come back to the bill's page (6.9), as the side buttons on the
    bill's page are. The side is not carried through signing in: it is taken on the bill's page after it.
  - Before the island has started, and with JavaScript off, a member's buttons are disabled; the title leads to the
    bill's page. While the island starts and reads the member's answer they keep their full colour, marked as waiting
    (`aria-busy`, a slow dark sheen), as the bill page's side buttons do; only with JavaScript off do they look dimmed.
  - A closed or hidden record, a bill the open data does not know and a vote's row have no buttons. The MP's own
    account takes no side (8.5.4): its rows show the numbers only.
  - Nothing changes in what is private: the member's side is only in their own answer, filled in the browser, and the
    page's HTML is the same for every member. `GET /me/bills` also says whether the caller has given the positions
    consent, so the row knows whether to ask.
- **The row lines up.** At every width the parts of a row share their edges; nothing depends on how a line happens to
  wrap, and nothing scrolls sideways at 390 px.
  - The organisations are a small list: "Organizacijos" on a line of its own, then one organisation per line. The
    stances form one column and the names another, so every name starts at the same place whatever the stance word's
    length; a long name wraps inside its own column, and "Nepatvirtinta" follows its name there.
  - The numbers, the bar and the two buttons have one width: the buttons together are exactly as wide as the bar.
    Where the row is one column (under 54rem) that block is as wide as the row up to 26rem; beside the title (wider
    screens) it is the side column.
  - A line of numbers breaks only between its parts, at the "·": a side's word and count stay together, and
    so does the threshold's part. Once the threshold is reached the line is "{n} dalyvių · suvestinė planuojama
    {yyyy-MM-dd}", "{n} dalyvių · suvestinė išsiųsta {yyyy-MM-dd}" or "{n} dalyvių · riba pasiekta", without the
    threshold's number (8.12.2, "The rows of a list").
- **Organisations** have no block of their own on the page: the footer's "Dalyvauti" links to `/organizacijos`, and a
  member registers one from "Mano organizacijos" on the account page. In the timeline they are named beside a bill,
  ordered as on the bill's page (for, against, remarks; within each, in the order published), never by size, and never
  counted.
- **A signed-in member** sees no introduction: the greeting (with the "needs JavaScript" note), "Sveiki sugrįžę", or
  "Sveiki prisijungę" with the start for a member who has not taken a side yet, between the greeting and the agenda
  (8.28.3); then "Jūsų projektai", a panel of the bills they follow, and only those, that are on the agenda or were voted on in the last
  week (all of them, not only those among the timeline's rows), each with its planned date, their own side ("Jūs
  prašote balsuoti už") or "Dar nenurodėte, ko prašote →" to the bill, the last week's vote's result and what is new on
  it (8.11.8); with none it says "Šiuo metu darbotvarkėje nėra projektų, kuriuos sekate.", and it ends with "Visi sekami
  projektai ir Seimo nariai →" to the account's list. The panel has no share link (the bill's page has it, 8.11.5).
  Then the same timeline, where under a bill's title the member's mark says "Sekate" on a followed bill, their own side
  or "Dar nenurodėte, ko prašote", and, on a followed bill, what is new there as a small link to it, the same line as
  the panel's (a bill in both shows it twice, the panel being the summary); on a bill where they took a side the pressed
  button shows it. A first side follows the bill and "Sekate" lights up in the row, with no line saying so. While
  sending is off (8.19.1) a note at the foot says "Vyksta uždaras bandymas: suvestinės sudaromos, bet Seimo nariams
  nesiunčiamos."
- **Privacy.** The page the server renders is the same public page for everyone. Everything that is the member's own
  (the panel, the marks in the rows, the sides) is filled in the browser from the member's own answers and is never
  rendered on the server or cached. Nothing shows who took which side or who follows what.
- **Data.** The agenda's public answer carries, for each bill, the organisations with a public statement on it
  (address, name, label, stance) and, once its threshold is reached, the summary its row names (8.12.2), so the page
  needs one public answer. The member's own side for the bills shown comes
  from `GET /me/bills`, at most 30 bills a request (the page shows twenty rows until a day grows, then as many requests
  side by side as the rows shown need). "Jūsų projektai" reads the member's follows (`GET /me/follows`) and asks
  `/me/bills` once more only about their followed bills that the rows do not show.
- **Cached.** The public answer the page is built from (the agenda with its counts and organisations) is the same for
  everyone, so the server keeps it in memory for 60 seconds (`FrontPageAgenda`) and builds the page from that: the most
  visited page costs the database one read a minute, not one per visitor. The numbers on the front page can therefore
  be up to a minute behind the bill's own page, which stays live. A side taken, changed or withdrawn marks the copy as
  out of date (`FrontPageFreshness`): the next request for the page reads the agenda again, so a person who takes a
  side and reloads sees the count they are part of. A copy is never replaced within two seconds of being made, so the
  page costs the database at most one read every two seconds however many sides are taken. Other changes (an
  organisation's statement, a moderator's action, a new planned date) wait for the minute. Where a member's row would
  still say "Niekas nenurodė, ko prašo" from the copy (a voted bill's), their own live answer takes that line away
  once they have a side, and a side column whose copy says nobody took part reads the live numbers once. The agenda
  page (8.11.1) reads the same copy, so a side marks it out of date for that page too. Nothing of a member's own is in
  that answer or in the cache. The HTML itself is answered `no-store`, because a page with an island carries a
  per-visitor antiforgery token (6.8); letting a proxy or a CDN cache visitors' pages needs that token moved out of the
  page first (9.1, section 12).
- The page has no row of links under the agenda: the footer, on every page, repeats the menu's sections (8.21).
