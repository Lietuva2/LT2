Interface style

Three bubbles, three speakers

JPOJIV

Do

  • Keep surfaces flat. Separate things with fill and space, then with a thin rule.
  • Cut one corner of the one sheet that matters most on a page, and of a portrait; nothing else. Buttons are straight.
  • Align left. Let long Lithuanian headings run in the condensed face instead of shrinking them.
  • Set anything a person wrote in the serif, inside a bubble, with their name above it; the bubble's tail points up to the name.
  • Pair every vote colour with its word.
  • Use the amber focus ring on every control.

Don't

  • No shadows, gradients, glows, rounded cards or pills. The speech bubbles' small radius is the one rounded shape.
  • No leaf green for "for" and no error red for "against": "for" is the links' green and "against" its matching red, and always the word.
  • No icons as decoration and no emoji. A word does the job.
  • No centred hero blocks and no rows of three equal feature cards.
  • No countdowns to a vote: "Planuojama …" only.
  • No turning logo on green, and only one on a page.

Prototype. Every MP, faction, bill and number in it is fictional. The fonts are served from the fonts/ folder, as they will be in the product.
