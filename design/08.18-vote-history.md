# 8.18 The history of the Seimas's votes

- **One page,** `/seimas/balsavimai` ("Seimo balsavimai"): public, server-rendered, readable without JavaScript, the
  same for everyone. It is the tab "Balsavimai" of "Projektai", in the menu item's dropdown and in the tab row under its
  heading (8.21). It is the long form of "Paskutiniai balsavimai" on the start page and of "recently voted" on the agenda
  page, and both link to it with "Ankstesni balsavimai →" beside that list's heading, also when the open data has
  no vote yet. The vote's page
  (`/seimas/balsavimai/{id}`) links up to it, and to its bill twice: the bill's number in the breadcrumb and a plain
  button, "Projekto {number} puslapis", on a sheet of its own above the result (owner, 2026-11-27). The sheet
  holds the vote's wording when there is one, the Seimas's outcome before its dash ("Pritarta", "Nepritarta", "Pritarta
  bendru sutarimu", "Sprendimas nepriimtas") as a neutral tag on its own line and the motion after it, capitalised, as text under it; then the bill's
  description under "Apie ką projektas", with the AI label as on the bill's page, when the link preview would carry it
  (8.13.1); then the button.
- **What it lists:** votes grouped by the day they were held (Vilnius day), the newest day first, within a day the
  latest first; each day with its date and weekday, as the days of the timeline are. A row links to the bill by its
  number and to the vote by the title. It shows the stage, the official note if there is one, and the result under
  "Seimas" in the right column ("už · prieš · susilaikė" with the bar, or "bendru sutarimu"). A vote
  that is not a stage result also says what it is (a vote on an amendment, a procedural vote, another vote) and shows
  its wording; a vote without a bill shows the agenda item's title in the bill's place. The bill's own vote list uses
  the same result column, with the date in each row and the vote's wording as its link (8.12.1).
- **The majority.** Every row of a vote with numbers says how it went, in a neutral chip before "už · prieš ·
  susilaikė": the majority of those who voted, "Dauguma už", "Dauguma prieš" or "Nėra daugumos", by the statistics
  page's rule (8.26.2, `AnalyticsRules.SeimasMajority`: more than half of for, against and abstain together); a
  consensus is "Pritarta bendru sutarimu", the data's own words; a vote without numbers shows no chip. The proceedings
  carry the official outcome in the vote's wording. The chip describes the ballot majority, calculated from the
  published totals. A majority against is
  the outlined chip and no majority the dashed one, so they differ at a glance without colour; no green or red. What
  the chip is, is one hint beside the list, "Kas yra balsavusiųjų dauguma?" (8.15), never one per row. The same chip
  is on the start page's "Paskutiniai balsavimai", the agenda page's recently voted, this list, the vote's page and the
  bill's page (its votes and the closing vote); the hint is beside this list, on the vote's page and on the bill's page,
  and the agenda's lists (the start page, the agenda page) carry none (owner, 2026-11-21).
- **No participants' numbers in the rows.** The bill's numbers at the time of an earlier vote are not kept, and
  today's numbers beside a vote held months ago would read as what people asked then. The bill's page shows both, and
  the closed tab of "Projektai" shows the numbers a record closed with.
- **Which votes:** one list to choose from, "Kurie balsavimai": "Projektų etapų balsavimai" (the default: what
  "Balsuota" shows, the stage results on bills), "Tik priėmimai" (the stage results of adoption) and "Visi balsavimai"
  (also amendments, procedural and other votes). A vote's kind and stage are the classification in force, a moderator's
  correction included (7.2).
- **Search:** one field that finds by the bill's number, official title or readable title, as every search of bills
  does (8.12.5, "How bills are searched"). Nothing found says so ("Nerasta nė vieno balsavimo.") and offers to clear.
- **Going back in time:** a date, "Iki" (a `yyyy-MM-dd` text field with a calendar button), shows the votes held on that day or earlier, so
  any month can be reached directly. Without it the list starts at the latest vote.
- **A part at a time:** the first 30 votes and "Rodyti daugiau", which adds 50 each time (8.16.4; the last press before
  300 adds what is left), with "Rodoma {n} iš {m}", as on the ballot list (8.17). One page shows at most 300; after that the link is "Senesni balsavimai →", which starts again from the day of
  the last vote shown ("Iki" set to it), or from the day before when all 300 fall on one day, since starting from that
  day would show the same 300 again. A day that is cut by the part shown simply continues when more is shown.
- **How it works:** the page's address carries the state (`?q=`, `?kas=etapai|priemimai|visi`, `?iki=YYYY-MM-DD`,
  `?rodyti=`): the controls are one GET form and the rest are links, so it works with JavaScript off and any view can
  be linked to; no island and no script. Unknown, malformed or oversized values (a search over 100 characters) fall
  back to the defaults, never to an error. The votes shown are read and counted in the database a part at a time, never
  all loaded to be cut in memory.
- **API:** `GET /api/v1/votes?q=&kind=stage-results|adoptions|all&until=&limit=&cursor=` (`limit` 30 by default, at most
  300), public, cached with an ETag like the rest of 7.4; it holds nothing about the caller. The page is built on the
  same answer.
- **The same pattern elsewhere.** `/projektai` (8.12.5) and an MP's votes (`/p/{slug}/balsavimai`: by day as here, the
  first 50, "Rodyti daugiau" adding 50 in place by `?rodyti=`, at most 300 on a
  page, then "Ankstesni balsavimai" from where the page ends, `?nuo=`; `GET /mps/{slug}/votes` takes `limit`, 50 by
  default, at most 300) grow the same way.
- **Surfaces (8.16):** the controls on the ground directly above the list; the list on a sheet. The sheet has no heading
  of its own, so the page's title and the tab row under it stay at the top of the screen, the days' dates just under them
  (8.16.5); the lead and the form scroll away.
- **Texts (8.15):** one short lead that says what the list is and where it comes from ("Dėl ko ir kaip balsavo Seimas –
  pagal dienas, nuo naujausių, iš Seimo atvirųjų duomenų."); the attribution and the invented-data notice as on every
  page of open data.
