# 8.24 How often a member's emails come

As MPs choose how often summaries come (8.19.4), every member chooses between an email about each thing that concerns
them and a gathered one, and how often.

- **One choice on the account page,** on its screen "Sekimas ir laiškai" (`/paskyra/sekimas`, 8.21; its own sheet
  `#laisku-daznis` after the switches), beside the switches (8.11.2, 8.14.14, 8.20.1, 8.23.9, 8.25), "Kaip dažnai siųsti
  laiškus" (the island `EmailRhythm`):
  - **"Iš karto"** (the default): within minutes. The follow job (`FollowJob` in the worker) runs four times an hour, at
    7, 22, 37 and 52 minutes past (`Jobs.FollowsCron`; the local stack sets `Jobs:FollowsCron` to every minute), and
    sends what happened since its last run. A person gets at most **one email per run**, gathering everything of that
    run for them: planned dates, votes and bills rejected or withdrawn (8.1 step 6), organisations' first statements, MPs' answers (8.20.1), comments under their
    arguments (8.23.9) and what followed members published (8.25). So a busy thread or a burst of answers makes one email
    a quarter of an hour, never a stream, and the email still carries no comment's text or writer and no answer's
    explanation.
  - **"Kartą per dieną"**: one morning email at `Follows:ResponsesAt` ("Rytinių laiškų laikas", 08:00, the hour of the
    daily and weekly emails) holding everything since the last one.
  - **"Kartą per savaitę ir artėjant balsavimui"**: one email on Monday at `Follows:ResponsesAt` holding everything since
    the last one, and what cannot wait (below).
- **What cannot wait.** A "planned" event whose planned sitting comes before the person's next gathered email is sent at
  once, in the next run of the follow job, for the daily choice as for the weekly one. Nothing else is sent early.
- **The switches stay** and say what is included: a kind switched off is in no email. Every email that gathers more than
  one thing has a part per kind in the switches' order, with each part's lines as 8.11.2, 8.14.14, 8.20.1, 8.23.9 and
  8.25 describe them, and ends, as every email of the rhythm, with the footer of 8.24.1 linking to the choice.
- **What it covers:** the follow emails (planned, voted, a bill rejected or withdrawn, organisations' first statements,
  MPs' answers, followed members' publications) and the notice of comments under one's arguments (8.23.9). **What it never delays:** sign-in and
  confirmation codes, notices about the account and its security (passkeys, known browsers, an MP's link and office,
  8.5), a moderator's statement about the person's own text or account (a hidden argument or comment, a writing
  restriction, an organisation's moderation), an answer to their feedback, and an MP's mail (8.19, its own rules). These
  always go at once, each on its own.
- **The rules of 8.11.2,** read for gathered emails:
  - once per event per person: an event is in one email only;
  - nothing from before the person followed; nothing about the person's side, the bill's counts or other people (the
    comments part says only that there are new comments under the person's arguments, 8.23.9; the members part names
    only the followed member, who allowed it, 8.25);
  - "nothing older than three days" is a rule about when the job first sees an event (a vote held more than three days
    before it was first seen is recorded and never sent); an event seen in time waits for the person's gathered email,
    so a weekly email can hold an event up to a week old: it is a list of what happened since the last email, and says
    so with each event's day;
  - one "planned" line per bill, with the date current when the email is made; a planned date already past by then is
    left out (the vote, if it came, is its own line); something no longer true when the email is made (a statement
    withdrawn, an answer hidden, a followed member's text withdrawn or hidden, a member whose profile is off) is left
    out.
- **The hour.** The daily and the weekly email go at the first run of the follow job from `Follows:ResponsesAt` on (at
  08:07 with the production schedule). The daily one holds what the job first saw before that day's 08:00 and no daily
  email has held; an event first seen at 08:07 waits for the next morning. The weekly one is the same from Monday's
  08:00, every Monday, whether or not the Seimas sits that week; the day is fixed, not a setting. A daily or weekly email
  is made once per period (`notify.rhythm_pass`, no person in it), also when the setting moves to a later hour the same
  day; a morning missed because the worker was down is made at the next run, late, rather than never.
- **What "comes before the next gathered email" means.** A "planned" date on or before the Vilnius day of the person's
  next gathered email, counted from when the job first saw the date: the email of the sitting's own morning is too late
  to act on. For the daily choice that is a sitting today or tomorrow; for the weekly one, any sitting up to and
  including the next Monday. It is decided once, when the date is first seen, and holds for any followed bill, with or
  without a side. If the date moves again within that window, the new date is sent early too (the limit of one
  "planned" email per bill per day is a rule of "Iš karto").
- **One email per person per run, whatever its parts.** Everything a run holds for a person goes in one email: the
  run's events under "Iš karto", the daily or the weekly email, and an early "planned" line; the early line is alone
  only when the run holds nothing else for the person. An email with one thing is the email that thing always has (its
  own subject and text, 8.11.2, 8.14.14, 8.20.1); with more, it is the gathered email (`GatheredNoticesEmail`): subject
  "Lietuva 2.0: naujienos apie tai, ką sekate" ("Lietuva 2.0: dienos naujienos apie tai, ką sekate" in the daily one,
  "Lietuva 2.0: savaitės naujienos apie tai, ką sekate" in the weekly one), a part per kind in the switches' order, each
  line with its day and its link, and at the end the footer of 8.24.1.
- **A change of the choice.** An event belongs to the choice the person had when the job first saw it, and comes in
  that choice's email, also after the choice has changed: what was waiting for Monday still comes on Monday after a
  switch to "Iš karto", and what comes after the switch comes at once. A second change before that email drops what
  was waiting under the first choice, unless the first was "Iš karto" (it had gone already). So no event is sent twice;
  at worst one is not sent, after two changes in a few days. Going back to the choice just left within 15 minutes (one
  run of the job, `RhythmRules.UndoWindow`) undoes the change as if it had not been made. The rules are `RhythmRules`
  (pure).
- **MPs' answers.** Recorded per answer (the bill, the MP, the moment the stance became public), not per bill and day:
  an answer the job first sees more than three days after it became public is recorded and never sent, as a vote.
  Under "Iš karto" the answers of a run are one part, listed per bill.
- **Auditing the choice.** A change is audited as `notices.frequency-changed` with the account as actor, the hashed
  address of the request and no details (neither the old nor the new choice). The switches are not audited.
- **Data and API.** `notify.notice_setting` holds, besides the switches, `frequency` (`immediate`, `daily`, `weekly`; no
  row means `immediate`), when the choice was made (`frequency_since`) and the choice before it with its own start
  (`previous_frequency`, `previous_since`), which the rule of a change needs; nothing else about the person.
  `notify.bill_event` records every kind's events (`planned`, `voted`, `closed`, `statement`, `response`, `member`,
  `comment`; no person and no text in it) and carries the sending marks: `outcome` and `sent_at` for the runs' emails, `daily_at`,
  `weekly_at` and `early_at`, so the emails find what is theirs without any per-person record of what was sent. Rows
  older than 30 days go with the daily outbox clean-up. The look-back for votes, closes, statements, MPs' answers and members'
  texts is 14 days, so a weekly email can still read what it reports. `GET /me/notices` carries `frequency`; the choice
  is read and changed at `GET|PUT /me/notices/frequency` (`{ "frequency": "immediate" | "daily" | "weekly" }`, error
  `notices.frequency-invalid`), its own route, so saving the switches and saving the choice never overwrite each other.
  It shares the per-account limit `RateLimits:Participation`. The choice is in the export with the switches.
- **A new kind of member email** joins the rhythm by declaring itself (`IMemberNoticeKind` in the Notifications module,
  and its part of the gathered email, `AddNoticePart`); the follow job (`NoticeDelivery`) is not edited for it. The
  kinds, in their parts' order: planned, voted, bills rejected or withdrawn (`closed`, under the "voted" switch, its part
  "Seimas nebesvarsto:" right after the votes), organisations' statements, MPs' answers, followed members' texts
  (8.25.5) and comments under one's arguments (8.23.9, 8.23.14).

## 8.24.1 Stopping them from the email

Members' emails have a one-click stop, like the MPs' summaries (8.19.4).

- **Which emails.** Every email that goes through the rhythm above: planned, voted, a bill rejected or withdrawn,
  organisations' statements, MPs'
  answers, followed members' texts, comments under one's arguments, and the gathered email. Not sign-in and
  confirmation codes, account and security notices, moderators' statements, answers to feedback or MP mail: those are
  not a list a person subscribed to, and they keep going.
- **What one press stops.** All of the account's notice emails: every switch of "Sekimas ir laiškai" (planned, voted,
  statements, MPs' answers, followed members, comments) is turned off. The follows stay, with their "Sekėjai" numbers
  and the person's own list. Turning switches on again on `/paskyra/sekimas` undoes it; the same link stops them again.
  Reason: a mail program's "Unsubscribe" means "stop this sender's list", and the gathered email mixes kinds, so
  stopping only the kind of the email in hand would leave the person getting the others under the same sender. The
  choice of how often (above) is left as it was.
- **In every such email.** The headers `List-Unsubscribe: <https://…/api/v1/notices/{token}/stop>` and
  `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058), on the member stream; and the footer below.
  Nothing is rewritten or tracked: each link is the address itself.
- **The footer.** Every email of the rhythm ends, under the rule in small grey type with the privacy line (8.22.3),
  with three lines: why it came, one sentence without a link ("Šį laišką gavote, nes sekate šį projektą Lietuva 2.0.",
  "…šią organizaciją…", "…šį narį…", "Šį laišką gavote, nes šis argumentas – jūsų." or "…šie argumentai – jūsų.", and
  in the gathered email "Šį laišką gavote pagal savo laiškų pasirinkimus Lietuva 2.0."); then "Keisti periodiškumą
  arba atsisakyti laiškų", where "Keisti periodiškumą" links to the choice of how often
  (`/paskyra/sekimas#laisku-daznis`, the sheet above) and "atsisakyti laiškų" to the stop page
  `/laiskai/nebegauti/{token}` (the page, never the API); then the privacy line. The plain-text part cannot hold links
  and gives the addresses: "Keisti periodiškumą: https://… · Atsisakyti laiškų: https://…". The email's body holds
  neither link. Other emails (codes, account and moderation notices, answers to feedback, MP mail) keep the privacy
  line alone.
- **The token.** One per account, derived with HMAC-SHA-256 under the mail key of 8.19.6 (`Security:MailHashKey`) from
  the account id and a generation (0), with a kind of its own, so it never equals an MP's token; 32 bytes, URL-safe.
  Every email carries the same link. Only its SHA-256 is kept, in `notify.member_mail_token` (account, hash, generation,
  when made), made when the account's first email of the rhythm is written; the clear token travels only in the outbox
  row's payload until the email is sent, as an MP's does. It does not expire. It goes with the account (and
  `IOutbox.ForgetAsync` takes unsent mail). It is not in the export: it is a key, not the person's data. The table is
  withheld from `lt2_readonly`.
- **The addresses.** The page `/laiskai/nebegauti/{token}` is server-rendered, without sign-in and without script: its
  GET changes nothing (mail systems' link scanners open links), and shows "Nebegauti laiškų", one sentence ("Paspaudę
  mygtuką nebegausite laiškų apie tai, ką sekate.") and a button "Nebegauti laiškų", a plain form posting back to the
  page. After it: "Laiškų apie tai, ką sekate, nebesiųsime. Vėl įjungti juos galite savo paskyroje." with the link to
  the follows screen. The one-click `POST /api/v1/notices/{token}/stop` (the page's button calls the same operation) is
  mapped outside the API group's same-origin and antiforgery filter exactly as the MP's one-click stop is, reads no
  body, can do nothing but turn the switches off, and is on `TermsExemptions.Routes`: stopping emails is never refused
  for unaccepted terms.
- **The same answer for every token.** An unknown, a malformed and a valid token get the same page and the same answer
  (204 from the API): nothing tells whether a link was valid or whose it is, and the page shows no name or address. A
  token of an account no longer a member changes nothing.
- **Limits.** Per client address and per token under the MP link's policies (`RateLimits:MpMail`,
  `RateLimits:MpMailToken`): no setting of its own. The page is not indexed, not stored, sends no referrer, and is left
  out of the usage list (8.27).
- **The second exception to "codes and tokens are not sent in addresses" (6.8),** for the reason of 8.19.4: the
  one-click form a mail program sends has nowhere else to put the token. Request logs and traces keep both paths with
  the token replaced (`Lt2.Hosting.SecretPaths`), as the proxy's filter does (deploy/Caddyfile).
- **Audit.** `notices.stopped-by-link` with the account as the subject (the target), no actor (whoever holds the link
  acted), the hashed address of the request and no details. Nothing is audited when every switch was already off. No
  confirmation email: there is nothing left to confirm into, and the account page shows the switches.
