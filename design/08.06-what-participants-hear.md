# 8.6 What participants hear

By email. The emails about what a member follows come in the member's rhythm (8.24): within minutes (one email per run
of the follow job), once a day, or once a week and when a vote approaches; each kind has its own switch on the follows
screen (`/paskyra/sekimas`):

- a followed bill entered the Seimas agenda ("Planuojama …", with its stage);
- the Seimas voted on a followed bill: the result (or that it was adopted by consensus), the Seimas's note, and how the
  MPs the member follows voted;
- MPs answered on a followed bill: the MPs whose response became public or changed its stance, with faction and stance,
  never the explanation (8.20.1);
- a followed organisation made a statement public (8.14);
- a followed member published an argument or a comment (8.25);
- someone commented under the member's own argument (8.23.9).

Moderation notices come at once and have no switch: the member's argument was published, hidden, merged or restored,
and a comment was hidden or restored, each with the moderator's statement when there is one.

**Not built.** An email when a followed bill was changed between stages.
