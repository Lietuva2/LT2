# 8.1 Life of an appeal

For the user the bill and the appeal are one thing: the bill's page (`/seimas/{number}`). "Kreipimasis" is the word for
what people on a bill ask of MPs; the Appeals module keeps it as the bill's record (5.3, 8.12.1). Registering, taking a
side and seeing it counted takes under two minutes on a phone.

1. **Take part.** On any active bill (8.12.1) a member takes a side (or never does), marks arguments of either side "Man
   svarbu" (counted only on their own side, 8.2), or writes one for either side, at once. The first side or argument
   creates the bill's record; nobody proposes, opens or approves anything, and nothing public says who was first. A new
   argument similar to an existing one is offered the existing one first. Positions can be changed until the bill closes.
2. **Describe.** A moderator writes or corrects what the bill is about (8.13) on any active bill, with a statement; the
   text is shown at once and enters the public history. Members do not write or propose descriptions.
3. **Moderate after the fact.** Nothing waits for a moderator before it is shown, except arguments that pre-screening or
   a report flags (8.3, 8.12.3).
4. **Summarise.** Once the bill reaches its threshold it enters the summaries sent to MPs (8.4), each reviewed by the
   moderators before it goes (8.19.2).
5. **Answer.** MPs publish responses; participants are told (8.6).
6. **Close.** The record closes, and its counts are frozen (nobody can take or change a side any more), when the Seimas has
   finished the bill:
   - **At the final vote,** the vote of its adoption stage (as classified, a moderator's correction included, 7.2): the
     hourly job closes it (`closed_reason` `vote`), and the page shows the Seimas result and how each MP voted beside
     the frozen counts.
   - **When the Seimas rejected the bill or its initiators withdrew it** before that stage (owner, 2026-11-19). The open
     data has no such status, so a moderator closes the record ("Uždaryti: projektas atmestas ar atsiimtas", 8.3) with
     the reason (`rejected` or `withdrawn`), the day of that decision (not in the future) and the source on lrs.lt
     (required, https on `lrs.lt` or a host under it), and a statement; under the bill's lock in one transaction, audited
     `record.closed` with the moderator as the actor. The page shows "Seimas projektą atmetė {data}." or "Iniciatoriai
     projektą atsiėmė {data}." with the link "Šaltinis" beside the frozen counts, and no MPs' votes. A moderator undoes a
     wrong close ("Atšaukti uždarymą", a statement, audited `record.reopened`): the record is open again with its live
     counts, since the positions were kept, and nobody is told. Such a record has no closing vote, so the analytics page
     does not compare it (8.26.2). A record closed by the vote is never reopened, and its closing vote stays the
     adoption-stage result: a moderator's correction of its classification out of that stage is refused (7.2).

   A member's first side on a bill follows it (8.11.2), so participants hear of the final vote as of every stage vote on a
   followed bill (`email.follow-voted`), and of a moderator's close by its counterpart (`email.follow-closed`, under the
   same switch and rhythm, 8.6, 8.24); there is no other closing email.

A bill passes several stages. The record and the positions stay the same through all of them.

**Not built.** Telling participants when a committee changes the bill, so they can reconsider (notify, do not reset).

**Not built: closing on the open data's word.** Once a live run of the Seimas service confirms which field of
`p2b.ad_sp_klausimo_svarstymo_eiga` (or the vote's wording) states that the Seimas rejected a bill or its initiators
withdrew it, the import closes the record with that reason and the vote's (or the decision's) date, as the moderator's
close does, and followers are told the same way; a moderator can reopen a wrong close. A bill "returned to the
initiators for improvement" (grąžintas iniciatoriams tobulinti) is not rejected and stays open. Until then the moderator's
close is the only way.
