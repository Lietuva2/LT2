# 8.19 Sending the summaries

How a summary really goes to MPs, who reviews it, how an MP changes or stops it without an account, which addresses are
never written to again, and how the introduction letter is sent. It adds to 8.4 and 8.7 and changes none of their rules.
While sending is off every summary is a dry run: made, reviewed and published, sent to nobody (8.4).

## 8.19.1 Three states of sending

- **One setting, `Digest:Sending`:** `off` (the default: a summary is prepared and reviewed the same way, then made as a
  dry run, nothing sent) or `automatic` (sent at its time). On the settings screen under "Suvestinės", changed by an
  administrator with a fresh passkey confirmation like every setting, audited, and shown on the methodology page
  (`/metodika#siuntimas`).
- **Nothing goes before the introduction letter (8.19.5).** While no introduction letter has been sent, summaries stay
  dry runs whatever the setting says (lock `no-introduction`), and the moderators' summaries screen says so.
- **Invented data never reaches a real mailbox.** The data is invented when the deployment says so (`Demo:Allowed`) or
  when the parliament data holds any synthetic stored response (`parliament.raw_document.synthetic`, whatever real rows
  stand beside it), so the lock does not depend on a flag someone can forget; Parliament answers it through the shared
  kernel. Where the data is invented, no MP mail (summaries, the introduction letter and its test copy, preference emails)
  is sent unless the deployment declares that all its mail is caught (`Email:Caught`, deployment configuration, false by
  default; the local `.env`, where Mailpit catches everything, sets it). Otherwise summaries stay dry runs (lock
  `not-caught`), the other MP mail is refused (`mp-mail.not-caught`), and the screens say so. The stand-in MPs' addresses
  are on a real domain, so this is a second lock beside the transport.
- **A run's status:** `prepared` (its bills and arguments chosen, under review, goes at its time; approved or not);
  `dry-run` (went while sending was off); `sent` (went: its deliveries are queued); `cancelled` (a moderator declined it,
  with a statement); `dropped` (at its time no bill was left: all held or no longer open; nothing went and nothing is
  public).
- **Which runs count.** For the weekly ceiling, "changed notably since the last summary", "covered by Monday's summary"
  and "never yet in a summary": a `prepared` run always (it is going to go); while sending is on, `sent` runs; while it is
  off, dry runs too. So the first real summary is not thinned by earlier dry runs, and a cancelled or dropped one frees
  its place for these counts. A cancelled or dropped run still counts as "already made" for its week or sitting, so a
  declined weekly summary is not made again the same week. An MP's delivery replaced by the letter (`introduced`) counts
  towards the ceiling. A bill counts as really summarised (`first_summarised_at`, 8.12.2) when a run that holds it
  becomes `sent`: the Appeals module sets it through `IDigestSource.MarkSummarisedAsync` in the sender's own transaction.

## 8.19.2 Reviewed a day ahead, not approved

- **Prepared ahead.** Each weekly and before-vote summary is prepared `Digest:ReviewHours` (a runtime setting, 24 by
  default, 1–72, on the settings screen and the methodology page) before its sending time, on the Vilnius clock: Monday
  07:00 is prepared on Sunday 07:00; 12:00 on the working day before a sitting is prepared at 12:00 the day before.
  Preparing fixes **which bills** (eligible and not held at that moment, by the rules of 8.4) and, per bill and side,
  **which arguments**: the two most endorsed among the published, not hidden arguments that were public by then, by
  counted marks only (8.2: those of people asking for that side; the count shown beside each is the same). Whether a
  moderator has seen one does not matter (8.3). A summary found after its time (the worker was down) is prepared and goes
  at once, on its sending day only. The worker's job (`digests`) runs every 15 minutes: it prepares what is due and lets
  go what is due, each under the summaries' lock.
- **The numbers are read when it goes,** not when it was prepared: both sides, the 30-day counts, the party groups
  (`GroupRules`), the surge note, the "Man svarbu" count beside each argument and the MP's own response. The snapshot and
  the public copy are made then. A bill held during review, or no longer open (closed by the final vote or by a moderator
  as rejected or withdrawn, 8.1 step 6, or its record hidden), drops out when it goes; a bill that became eligible after the preparation waits for the next summary. With no
  bill left nothing goes and the run is `dropped`.
- **Hiding brings the next argument in.** During review a moderator hides an argument from the summaries screen with the
  normal action (8.3: a statement, audited, the author told; hidden on the bill's page too). The summary then takes the
  next argument of that side by "Man svarbu" now, from the same pool (public when it was prepared, not hidden), never one
  chosen before; the screen marks it "Įtrauktas vietoj paslėpto". The chosen list is stored (`digest_choice`) and stays as
  it is apart from that: it is not sorted again by marks when it goes, so the list the screen shows is the one that goes
  (the screen orders by the marks now). An argument that leaves the published ones any other way (withdrawn, merged) is
  replaced the same way.
- **Approving and declining.** A prepared summary offers two decisions, each needing a moderator's staff-passkey session
  (6.3), taken under the summaries' lock so two presses act once, and audited with the moderator, whom no public answer
  names:
  - "Patvirtinti": the summary is marked reviewed (`approved_at`, `approved_by`). It still goes at its own time; nothing is
    sent earlier, and hiding arguments and "Sulaikyti siuntimą" stay possible until then. It asks for no fresh passkey
    confirmation, since it sends nothing. The screen then says "Patvirtinta {yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm}" beside "Išeis …".
  - "Nesiųsti" with a statement of at least 20 characters, public on the copy: the summary is `cancelled`; its copy shows
    what it held at that moment and says "Nesiųsta" with the statement.
  - If nobody does either, it goes at its time as prepared.
- An urgent summary is approved by the moderator who asks for it, which is its review (8.4), and it goes at once.
- **Moderators are told.** At preparation one email (outbox kind `email.digest-review`, "Suvestinė Seimo nariams išeis
  …", through `EmailLayout.Build`) goes to every account holding an activated moderator or administrator role: which
  summary, when it goes, how many bills and arguments, what they may do, and the link to `/moderavimas/suvestines`;
  never an argument's text or anything about an MP. And on **every page**, for such an account (by its role, whatever its
  session), a box under the header while a prepared summary nobody approved or declined has not gone: "Suvestinė Seimo
  nariams išeis {yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm}: {n} projektų, {m} argumentų." with "Peržiūrėti" (the counts with their Lithuanian
  forms: "1 projektas", "3 projektai"), linking to the screen. As the MP's offer (8.5.1), the served markup is hidden and
  the same for every member; the browser fills and shows it from the account's own `GET /me` (`digestReview`, given to
  staff accounts only; the server-rendered page holds nothing of it). "Patvirtinti" or "Nesiųsti" takes the box away for
  every staff account; it is not shown on the summaries screen itself.
- **The summaries screen** `/moderavimas/suvestines` (a staff-passkey session; `<PasskeyStep Staff="true">` otherwise),
  linked from the moderation queue, lists the prepared summaries first, soonest first, then the latest gone. Each shows
  its text common to every MP once, as MPs read it: the bills with their numbers now (a prepared one) or as it went, the
  chosen arguments with "Paslėpti" (a prepared one), each bill's "Sulaikyti siuntimą" or "Atnaujinti siuntimą", how many
  MPs it would go to now or went to, and what happened. One line says that the party group numbers, the MP's own response
  and the personal links differ per MP; there is no per-MP preview.
- Recipients are worked out when it goes: preferences, the suppression list and the ceiling as they are at that moment.
- **The public copy (8.7) says what happened**, in one line under the date: "Bandomoji suvestinė: niekam nesiųsta",
  "Išsiųsta Seimo nariams: {n}" with the time, or "Nesiųsta" with the moderator's statement. Never which MPs. A prepared or
  dropped summary has no public copy.

## 8.19.3 What is sent, and to whom

- **Recipients:** every sitting MP with an official address (8.4, 8.5.1: the same definition), except: the address is on
  the suppression list (8.19.6); the MP's preference excludes this kind (8.19.4); the MP has not had the introduction
  letter yet (8.19.5: the letter goes instead); over the weekly ceiling.
- **One delivery row per run, MP and recipient** (`official`; `extra` for a confirmed extra recipient; `office` for an
  office member's copy): `dry-run` (the run went while sending was off), `queued`, `sent`, `failed` (gave up after the
  retries), `bounced`, `skipped-ceiling`, `skipped-preference`, `suppressed`, `introduced` (got the introduction letter
  instead). The row holds the MP's party numbers as they went, and no address.
- **The address** is read from open data when the delivery is queued and travels only in the outbox row, which is
  deleted when sent. Parliament gives addresses out through one shared-kernel call made for sending only
  (`IMpMailAddresses`). No log line holds an address (6.5).
- **The text** is the dry run's (appendix A), as light HTML through `EmailLayout.BuildMp`, with a text part:
  - per MP, the bill order of 8.4: bills where the MP's committee leads, then bills planned in the coming days, then by
    participants. Lead committees are not imported, so that group is always empty and the rest holds;
  - per bill, the MP's own response (8.5.5) when one is published: "Jūsų atsakymas: balsuosiu už ({date}). Keisti arba
    papildyti: {link}"; otherwise "Atsakyti vieną kartą visiems: {link}" to the bill's page, where a linked account
    answers once for everyone; then "Visi skaičiai ir argumentai: {link}";
  - at the end: what the numbers are and are not, the public copy's link, the preferences link (8.19.4), and one
    sentence on taking over the profile by signing in with this address (8.5.1);
  - **a long argument is shortened:** its beginning by the same pure function as the page (`TextExcerpt`: about 300
    characters plus a margin of 100, cut at a word boundary), then "…", the "Man svarbu" count, and under it a plain line
    "Visas argumentas: {link}". The link is a full address (`SiteLinks.Absolute`, no tracking parameters) to that
    argument on the summary's public copy (`/suvestines/{day}#reason-{run}-{bill}-{side}-{place}`): the public copy
    always exists for a run that sends and shows the snapshot's text, while the bill's page may since have hidden or
    withdrawn the argument. The snapshot keeps the whole text. The email prints no other member-written text: the MP's
    own response appears as its stance and date only.
- Organisations stay out of summaries (8.14.11).
- **Headers:** `List-Unsubscribe` with the one-click form (RFC 8058) and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`. No tracking pixel, no
  rewritten links. A copy to an extra recipient carries `List-Unsubscribe` with its own "Nebegauti kopijų" page and no
  `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, so the one-click route stays the only write of MP mail without the same-origin check (a
  member's stop link, 8.24.1, is the one other, outside MP mail).
- **A separate stream:** MP mail has its own sender address (`Email:Mp:FromAddress`) and may have its own SMTP account
  (`Email:Mp:Smtp`, falling back to the common one), so trouble with it cannot block sign-in codes. Deployment
  configuration, like the rest of `Email`.
- The outbox retries as for any email. A permanent refusal of the recipient (an SMTP 5xx answer, `RecipientNotAccepted`)
  marks the delivery `bounced` and suppresses the address (8.19.6); a refused sender or a 4xx answer is retried.
- **Copies for the MP's office.** Every office member of a linked MP (8.5.3) gets a copy of each summary that goes for
  that MP, at their own account's address, in the copy's format ("Tai Seimo nario suvestinės kopija, siunčiama jums kaip
  jo biuro nariui. Seimo narys: {0}. Kaip dažnai gauti kopijas, pasirenkate savo paskyroje: {1}"), whatever the MP chose
  for their own summaries. Each member has their **own frequency** (the same four choices, default "Kas savaitę ir prieš
  balsavimus"), kept in Notifications (`notify.office_copy`, one row per member and MP; no row is the default) and changed
  on "Dalyvavimas" (`GET|PUT /api/v1/me/mp/office/copies`) for themselves only, in any session, since it is the member's
  own mail and does not speak for the MP. The MP stopping their summaries does not stop the office's, and the other way
  round.
- Each copy has its own delivery row (`recipient` = `office`, the member's account in `office_user_id`, never the address),
  its own weekly ceiling (`Digest:MaxPerWeek`, counted per member; the MP's own count is unchanged), and the suppression
  list applies to the member's address. The copy carries `List-Unsubscribe` with the one-click form to the same route as
  the MP's (`POST /api/v1/mp-mail/{token}/stop`), with the member's own token: it stops that member's copies and nothing
  else, so the one write outside the same-origin check stays one route; the email's own "Nebegauti kopijų" line opens the
  copies' page (`/suvestines/kopijos/{token}`). The token is derived from the member's account, the MP and a generation
  under the mail key, and only its hash is kept on the member's row; both paths are kept out of logs.
- Sending off (a dry run) or a lock of 8.19.1 sends no copies either; an urgent summary is copied like the others.
- Ending the office link (the member leaves or is removed, the MP gives the profile up or an administrator ends it, the
  account is deleted) ends the copies and deletes the member's choice in the same transaction (`IOfficeCopiesEnd`, called by
  Identity; the account's deletion through Notifications' account data). Addresses come only from Identity's `IMpLinks`
  (`OfficeMembersAsync`), for the outbox row.
- Never public: no public copy, page, API answer or log says that office members exist or that copies went; the public
  copy's "Išsiųsta Seimo nariams: {n}" and the screen's MP count count MPs only.

## 8.19.4 Preferences without an account

- Every email to an MP carries a link to **"Suvestinių nustatymai"**, `/suvestines/nustatymai/{token}`: one
  server-rendered page for that MP, with forms and no script. It shows the MP's name and the choice: "Kas savaitę ir prieš
  balsavimus" (the default), "Tik kas savaitę", "Tik prieš balsavimus", "Nesiųsti". An urgent summary counts as "prieš
  balsavimus".
- **The token** is derived (HMAC-SHA-256 under the mail key of 8.19.6, of the MP's person number and the link's
  generation), so every email carries the same link, while only its SHA-256 is stored, from the moment the first email
  to that MP is queued. It does not expire, so the link in an old email keeps working. It opens only this page and the
  one-click stop: no account, no session, no other data. The page is not indexed and sends no referrer.
- **One-click stop:** the `List-Unsubscribe` address accepts a POST with the token and sets "Nesiųsti". It is the one
  write of MP mail that needs no same-origin check, because mail programs send it; it can do nothing else (a member's
  stop link, 8.24.1, is the one other such write, on the same pattern).
- **Every change is confirmed by email to the official address**, with the new choice and the link, so a change made by
  someone the email was forwarded to is seen and undone. "Nesiųsti" is confirmed once too, and after it nothing more is
  sent to that MP (the introduction letter included) except what the MP asks for from this page. While the official
  address is missing or suppressed, a frequency change is saved without a confirmation.
- **A new link.** The page offers "Atsiųsti naują nuorodą": the link moves to the next generation, the old token stops
  working and a new link goes to the official address. For an MP who suspects the link has travelled.
- **An extra recipient** (an assistant): the page takes one address; a new one replaces the one before. A confirmation
  link goes to the official address (a random token, stored hashed, 24 hours, one use, `extra-confirm`); only after it is
  confirmed do copies go to the extra address, which is then told so by one email with its own stop link. Each copy says
  whose summary it is and carries its own link "Nebegauti kopijų" (a token kind of its own, `extra-stop`, one per
  confirmed extra address), which removes the extra address. The MP removes it on the page. The address is stored as
  written, shown only on that MP's page, and deleted when removed. A copy goes only when the MP's own email went.
- A new link or an extra recipient is refused (`mp-mail.no-address`) while the official address is missing or
  suppressed, since its email could reach nobody.
- **The confirmation and a copy's stop** open `/suvestines/nustatymai/patvirtinti/{token}` and `/suvestines/kopijos/{token}`:
  opening either changes nothing, a button does (a form, no script), because mail scanners open links.
- These paths and the preferences page are kept out of logs by replacing the token (`Lt2.Hosting.SecretPaths` for the
  application's logs and traces, a `regexp` filter in the Caddyfile). An unknown, replaced and wrong token all get the
  site's "not found" page (`mp-mail.not-found` in the API).
- **A linked account (8.5: the MP's account or an office member)** sees the same choice on "Dalyvavimas", in the MP
  part's "Suvestinės el. paštu", and reads and changes it there without the token, in a passkey session only
  (`GET|PUT /api/v1/me/mp/mail`); the extra address is not shown there.
- Whether an MP stopped the summaries is never public (8.4). Staff see a delivery's status; nobody else does.

## 8.19.5 The introduction letter

- **Written and signed by a person.** An administrator writes it on the screen "Pristatymo laiškas"
  (`/administravimas/pristatymas`): subject, text in plain paragraphs (with lists, as every typed text: 8.2, "A text's
  layout"), the signer's name and role. The system adds below the signature what it adds to every MP email: the
  preferences link and the sentence on taking over the profile.
- The administrator previews it, sends a test copy to the administrator's own address (marked "[Bandomoji kopija]", the
  personal links replaced by an example), and then sends it to every sitting MP with an official address who has had
  none, is not on the suppression list and has not chosen "Nesiųsti": one press with the number of MPs in the
  confirmation and a fresh passkey confirmation. Audited. It is a run of kind `introduction`, with deliveries like any
  other.
- **Once per MP.** An MP who joins the Seimas later gets the current letter in place of the first summary that would
  have gone to them (`introduced`), and summaries from the next one on.
- A changed text is a new version; it goes only to MPs who have had none. Versions already sent stay stored. Saving the
  letter unchanged makes no new version.
- **Public:** `/suvestines/pristatymas` shows the letter as sent, with its date and signer, and earlier versions. The
  summaries' list links to it. Each version's text shows what changed against the version before it, the letter in force
  first, the oldest whole, the text before each change under "Rodyti tekstą prieš pakeitimą" (8.16.2); each keeps its
  own subject and signer.

## 8.19.6 Addresses never written to again

- `notify.suppression`: a keyed hash of the address, the reason (`bounced`, `complaint`, `staff`), the date, and for a
  hand-made entry the administrator and the statement. No address.
- **Added automatically** when a receiving server refuses an MP email for good (a permanent SMTP refusal).
- **Not built.** A provider's reports of later bounces and complaints (the reason `complaint`) wait for an email provider
  to be chosen (12, "Email provider").
- **By hand:** an administrator adds an address (typed, stored only hashed) or removes an entry, each with a statement,
  audited. The screen "Nesiunčiama" (`/administravimas/nesiunciama`) lists the sitting MPs whose official address is
  suppressed, with reason and date, and counts the other entries without showing them; an entry that is not a listed MP
  is removed by typing its address.
- It covers MP mail only: summaries, the introduction letter, copies and preference emails. Sign-in codes are not
  affected.
- The key is handled as the IP hash's is (a secret file, HMAC-SHA-256), but it is its own secret, `mail_hash_key`
  (`Security:MailHashKey`), because the IP key may be rotated (6.5) and a suppressed address must stay suppressed. The
  same key derives the MPs' and office members' links and the members' stop links (8.24.1), so changing it breaks the
  links in emails already sent too.

## 8.19.7 Data and API

`notify`: `digest_run.status` takes the values of 8.19.1, with `due_at`, `sent_at`, `approved_by`, `approved_at`,
`cancelled_by`, `cancelled_at`, `statement`, `mp_count`, `sent_count`; kind `introduction` with the letter's version
(`letter_version`). `digest_choice` (8.19.2): per prepared run, the chosen arguments by id with their record, side,
place, whether one came in for a hidden one, and when one left; no author. `digest_delivery`: key (run, MP, recipient,
office member), the statuses of 8.19.3, the MP's party numbers; `office_user_id` names an office member's account for
recipient `office`. `office_copy`: each office member's own choice of copies and the hash of their stop token (8.19.3).
`mp_email_pref`: per Seimas person: frequency, extra address, its confirmation, the link's and the extra address's
generations, changed time. `mp_mail_token`: hashed token per MP and kind (`preferences`, `extra-confirm`, `extra-stop`),
expiry and use for `extra-confirm`. `introduction_letter`: version, subject, text, signer, who wrote it, sent time.
`suppression` as in 8.19.6. All withheld from the read-only role.

```
GET    /api/v1/moderation/digests                  moderator, staff passkey: the summaries screen
POST   /api/v1/moderation/digests/{id}/approve     moderator, staff passkey ("Patvirtinti"; sends nothing)
POST   /api/v1/moderation/digests/{id}/cancel      moderator, staff passkey; statement
GET    /api/v1/mp-mail/{token}                     the MP's name and choice
PUT    /api/v1/mp-mail/{token}                     frequency
POST   /api/v1/mp-mail/{token}/stop                one-click (RFC 8058)
POST   /api/v1/mp-mail/{token}/renew               a new link to the official address
PUT    /api/v1/mp-mail/{token}/extra               ask for an extra recipient
DELETE /api/v1/mp-mail/{token}/extra
POST   /api/v1/mp-mail/extra/confirm               from the link sent to the official address
POST   /api/v1/mp-mail/extra/stop                  from a copy
GET|PUT /api/v1/me/mp/mail                         a linked account, passkey session
GET|PUT /api/v1/me/mp/office/copies                an office member's own copies, any session
GET|PUT /api/v1/admin/introduction                 administrator
POST   /api/v1/admin/introduction/test             to the administrator's own address
POST   /api/v1/admin/introduction/send             fresh passkey confirmation
GET|POST|DELETE /api/v1/admin/suppression          administrator
GET    /api/v1/digests/introduction                public
```

The token endpoints are limited per address and per token like the sign-in ones (6.4: `RateLimits:MpMail`,
`RateLimits:MpMailToken`), and answer the same for an unknown and a wrong token.

## 8.19.8 Done when

The tests of sending (`MpSendingTests`, `MpMailTests`) hold these:

- With sending off: dry runs, nothing in the outbox for MPs.
- With sending on, a summary is prepared a day ahead, the moderators are told, and it goes at its time, approved or not;
  each MP's mailbox gets one email with both sides, the MP's own response where there is one, and working links; a
  declined or dropped one sends nothing and frees the ceiling.
- No summary goes before the introduction letter; with invented data none goes unless the mail is declared caught.
- An MP changes the frequency, stops by the page and by the mail program's one-click, renews the link, and adds and
  removes an extra recipient confirmed from the official address; each is confirmed by email; the next summaries follow
  the choice.
- A permanently refused address gets no further MP mail; an administrator can add and remove entries.
- The public copy says what happened to the summary and never which MPs received it; no page, API answer or log shows
  an MP's choice or an address.
- A simulated month of agenda changes (8.4) holds with preferences, suppression, the ceiling and review in play.
