# 8.21 The menu, the footer and the account item

- **The menu:** the logo, "Projektai", "Seimo nariai" (`/seimo-nariai`), "Statistika" (`/statistika`, the analytics page
  of 8.26) and "Prisijungti" for a visitor or the account item for a member. `MenuSections` holds the items, one list
  with a line for each that says what its page is (8.28.2), and decides which one a page belongs to. The menu, the
  dropdown, the tab row, the footer and "Kur ką rasti" (8.28.4) are drawn from that list.
- **"Projektai" has a dropdown of its four tabs:** "Darbotvarkė" (`/darbotvarke`), "Projektai" (`/projektai`),
  "Balsavimai" (`/seimas/balsavimai`, 8.18) and "Suvestinės" (`/suvestines`). Where the device can hover (a mouse),
  "Projektai" is a link to the agenda and the dropdown shows on hover, after a moment, and stays a moment after the
  pointer leaves; it also shows while the keyboard's focus is in the item, so Tab reaches the entries, and Escape hides
  it. On a touch screen it is a disclosure built like the account item, opened by a press. Both work without script; the
  same small script (`lt2-account.js`) adds Escape and closes the disclosure on a press outside and focus leaving. The
  device decides, not the width: a narrow window with a mouse has the hover. Each entry shows its line under its name (8.28.2). The current page is marked in it. The item itself is marked current on the four pages and on
  everything under them (a bill and its history, a vote, a summary's date page, the introduction letter's public page).
  The link under the agenda's voted list (8.18) is a second way to the history of votes.
- **The tab row:** the same four tabs stand as a row of plain links under the heading of the four pages, the current one
  marked; no script (`ProjectTabs`). It stands on the summaries' list, not on a date's page, and on the history of votes
  whatever its search, not on a vote's page (`/seimas/balsavimai/{id}`): there "Projektai" is current, no dropdown item
  is marked as the page, and the vote's breadcrumb leads up to the history. Addresses do not depend on the tabs.
- **"Seimo nariai"** is marked current on the list and on everything under it (a profile and its votes, a faction, a
  committee); "Statistika" on its own page.
- **One line from 340 px.** Under 32rem the logo stands without the words "Lietuva 2.0" (still read by screen readers)
  and the items take the secondary type size (15 px); under 26rem the gaps close up, the mark is a little smaller
  (2.25rem) and the arrows sit closer; under 24rem the items take the smallest type size (13 px).
- **The account item** reads "Paskyra", not the person's name, so the page's HTML is the same for every member. It is a
  disclosure that works without script, holding "Mano paskyra" and "Atsijungti". With script, the opened item also shows
  the person's display name (or, without one, the account's own address) at the top and the links their account has,
  read from `GET /me` in the browser; nothing about the person is in the page as served:
  - "Sutikti su naudojimo taisyklėmis" while the terms in force are not accepted (8.22.2);
  - "Moderavimas" and "Atsiliepimai" (8.10) for any active staff role; "Nustatymai", "Kvietimai", "Paskyros" (the full lock of an account, 8.3), "Lankomumas" (8.27) and "Foniniai darbai" (the
    jobs dashboard, a full page load) for an active administrator only, since those screens refuse the others;
  - "Projektai, dėl kurių į jus kreipiamasi" for an MP's account and an MP's office member (the page serves both, 8.5.7);
  - for a staff account whose session cannot act as staff (`staffAccount` with no `activeStaffRoles`), "Prisijungti
    darbuotojo prieigos raktu" in the place of the staff links: the page of 6.2 that makes the session a staff session,
    which then goes back to the page the item was opened on.

  It closes on Escape, on a press outside and when focus leaves it.
- **The account area is four screens under `/paskyra`.** Every screen has the same title ("Sveiki, …!") and under it a
  row of links to the four screens, the current one marked (`aria-current`), styled like the tab row of "Projektai", no
  script, wrapping on a phone (`AccountTabs`). A visitor who opens one is sent to sign in and comes back to the same
  screen. The screens and what each holds:
  - **"Profilis" (`/paskyra`):** what others may see of the person and what they say of themself. The level and roles,
    for an MP's official address one line about the MP (8.5.1), then one form with one "Išsaugoti" (8.28.5): the display
    name (6.2), "Apie mane" and the box "Viešas profilis" with the link to the public page while it is on (8.25), party
    support (5.2), with the box "Rodyti viešame profilyje" while the profile is public (8.25.9).
  - **"Sekimas ir laiškai" (`/paskyra/sekimas`):** what the person follows and the email switches (`#sekami`), then how
    often the emails come (`#laisku-daznis`, 8.24).
  - **"Dalyvavimas" (`/paskyra/dalyvavimas`):** the MP part (8.5; `#mp-offer`, `#mp-link`), organisations, the
    invitation code (`#my-invitation`), and the staff part: a granted role's activation and, for a staff account whose
    session is not a staff session, one sentence and the staff passkey step of 6.2, in place. The staff screens are
    reached from the account item only.
  - **"Profilis" (`/paskyra`):** a profile switched on under an earlier consent text stays on and its form shows, under the
    switch, one line with a way to read the text in force and accept it (8.25.1, owner 2026-11-19); the page asks `GET /me`.
  - **"Saugumas ir duomenys" (`/paskyra/saugumas`):** the email address and changing it (`#el-pastas`, 6.2), passkeys,
    sessions, known browsers (`#zinomos-narsykles`), the positions consent (a consent about stored data, withdrawn with
    what it covers; shown for any consent not withdrawn, one to an older text with a line to read and accept the text in
    force, 6.5), the export and deleting the account (`#my-data`), signing out.

  While the terms in force are not accepted, the note about them (8.22.2) stands under the row on every screen: it
  concerns the whole account. Emails and hints link to the anchors on their screen's address:
  `/paskyra/sekimas#sekami` (the front page's list), `/paskyra/sekimas#laisku-daznis` (every follow email's footer,
  8.24.1), `/paskyra/saugumas#zinomos-narsykles` (the known browsers' notice, 8.20.2), `/paskyra/saugumas#my-data` (the
  privacy notice, the terms' acceptance screen), `/paskyra/dalyvavimas#my-invitation`. Each screen is answered
  `no-store`, as every page to a signed-in session (6.8), and an island on it is disabled until it starts, with
  `<NeedsScript />`.
- **The footer** repeats the menu's sections and holds what has no place in the menu, in three short groups, then the
  theme switch and the line "Lietuva 2.0 · uždaras bandymas". Plain links, no script, nothing marked current (the menu
  does that); the groups wrap on a phone.
  - "Naršyti": the menu's items in the menu's order and words, so a reader at the end of a long page goes on without
    scrolling back to the top: "Darbotvarkė", "Projektai", "Balsavimai", "Suvestinės", "Seimo nariai", "Statistika".
    The account item and "Prisijungti" are not repeated.
  - "Apie": "Metodika" (`/metodika`), "Moderavimo taisyklės", "Naudojimo taisyklės", "Privatumas" (8.22).
  - "Dalyvauti": "Organizacijos", "Atsiliepimai".

  "Kaip tai veikia" is the introduction on the front page, not the methodology page.
- **The theme switch** stands in the footer, so a visitor has it too. It is hidden until its script shows it, so it is
  absent without script.
