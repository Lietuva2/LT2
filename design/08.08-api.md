# 8.8 API

The routes under `/api/v1`, with who may call each. Routes that have a list in their own section are listed there:
the Seimas data (7.4), organisations (8.14), sending to MPs, MP mail and the introduction letter (8.19.7), comments
(8.23.11), public profiles (8.25), analytics (8.26) and usage (8.27).

Who may call: **public** (anyone, no session); **signed in** (any session, also one whose registration is not finished);
**member** (a signed-in member); **moderator** (an active moderator or administrator role in a staff-passkey session,
6.2); **administrator** (an active administrator role in a staff-passkey session). Every write needs antiforgery and the
Origin check, except the two one-click stops (8.19.4, 8.24.1). A member's own answers are never cached.

The answers about a bill are keyed by its number and return the empty open state (no record id, zero counts) for an
active bill nobody has taken part in, not "not found"; a finished bill nobody took part in is "not found"
(`record.not-found`).
