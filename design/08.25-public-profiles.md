# 8.25 A member's public profile, and following a member

A member may say publicly who they are ("Apie mane"), with no length of its own and with links and their own contacts
allowed. A member who chooses a public profile gets a page of their own, reached by pressing their name beside their
texts, with a quick card on hover; the page is indexed by search engines, and other members may follow the person. One
switch, "Viešas profilis", is the person's consent to all of it. The member's texts on the page are their arguments and
the comments they wrote in their own voice (8.23), and following a member sends a line for either, in the follower's
rhythm (8.24). Changing the display name is in the account design (6.2), with no limit on how often.

**Why only by choice.** Without a public profile nothing public gathers one person's texts: no public answer carries an
account's id (8.23.11), and display names, the only thing beside a text, are neither unique nor stable. 6.1 names who
took which side as the worst realistic leak, and 8.2 keeps anything public from telling whether an argument is on its
author's side. A stable public address beside each of a person's texts lets anyone collect everything one person wrote
by searching for that address, with a crawler or a search engine, and a list of one person's arguments across bills is,
in aggregate, a picture of their political views even though no single argument reveals a side (the inference 6.1 and
the minimisation of 6.5 guard against). So this happens only for a member who chooses it, and then fully: the page is
indexed and the person can be followed.

## 8.25.1 One switch, by the member's choice

- **A public profile exists only for a member who switched it on.** The account screen "Profilis" (`/paskyra`, 8.21)
  holds the switch "Viešas profilis", off until chosen. Everything a member writes is written under the rule that nothing
  gathers one person's texts, so gathering it needs the person's own decision (purpose limitation); and the gathered
  list is the picture described above.
- **"Profilis" is one form with one "Išsaugoti" and one request** (`MyProfile`, `PUT /me/profile`, 8.28.5): the display
  name, "Viešas profilis ir „Apie mane“" (the text "Apie mane" and the box "Viešas profilis") and party support with the
  box "Rodyti viešame profilyje" (8.25.9), offered while the form's "Viešas profilis" is ticked.
- **The switch is the consent, and covers everything.** Switching it on is recorded as a consent of the kind
  `public-profile`, with its text version (`PublicProfileConsent.TextVersion`, `2026-11-16`; the form sends the version
  it showed, `ProfileLimits.ConsentVersion`, and another is refused with `profile.consent-outdated`), in
  `identity.consent`, as the terms (`terms`) and the positions consent (`positions`) are (5.2, 8.22.2). A changed text
  gets a new version; a profile switched on under an earlier version stays on (the earlier consent covered it), and the
  form asks for the consent only when the profile is switched on. **A profile on under an earlier text is asked once, on the
  account page** (owner, 2026-11-19, as 6.5 asks for the positions consent): `GET /me` says to the person alone
  (`publicProfileConsentOutdated`) that the profile is on and no `public-profile` consent to the text in force stands; then
  "Profilis", under the switch, has one line, "Sutikote su ankstesne šio teksto versija. Perskaitykite galiojančią ir
  patvirtinkite.", and "Skaityti galiojantį tekstą", which opens the text in force in place with a box and "Sutinku"
  (`PUT /api/v1/me/profile/consent`, the version shown: another is refused with `profile.consent-outdated`). Accepting ends the
  earlier consents and records one to the version in force in one transaction, audited without details, and changes nothing
  else (the profile, its address and its followers stay); the line goes. Switching the profile off stays one step with the line
  showing, and withdraws the consent as always; a profile that is off, or on under the text in force, has no line. No email and
  no banner elsewhere ask for it; the profile keeps working unaccepted, so nothing the person has is held back or deleted. Before
  saving, the form says everything it covers (`UiText` `PublicProfile.Consent`):
  "Jūsų profilio puslapyje bus jūsų vardas, „Apie mane“ ir viskas, ką viešai parašėte (argumentai ir komentarai),
  surinkta į vieną vietą su nuorodomis; prie visų jūsų argumentų, komentarų ir „Sutinku“ bus nuoroda į jį. Puslapį gali
  rodyti paieškos sistemos. Kiti nariai galės jus sekti ir gaus laišką, kai paskelbsite argumentą ar komentarą;
  puslapyje bus rodomas sekėjų skaičius, bet ne tai, kas jus seka (to nesužinosite ir jūs). Išjungus profilį, puslapis
  dingsta iš karto, bet paieškos sistemų kopijos išnyksta tik joms vėl apsilankius. Išsaugodami įjungtą profilį sutinkate
  su tuo."
  There is no separate permission to be followed: a public profile can be followed.
- **Who may switch it on:** a member with a display name (`profile.name-required`: a page needs a name to show; emptying
  the name while the profile is on is refused the same way). The MP's own account is refused (`mp.takes-no-part`, 8.5.4);
  office members are not.
- **Switching it off withdraws the consent** and, at once, in one transaction: removes the page and every link to it from
  every text, ends every follow of the person (`IMemberFollowEnd`; followers are not told why; the member is gone from
  their list), withdraws the party shown (8.25.9), and retires the address: it is never used again, and switching the
  profile on later makes a new one, so old links and search results lead nowhere on the site. Before saving the form
  says so ("Išsaugojus profilis išsijungs iš karto: puslapis ir nuorodos prie argumentų, komentarų ir „Sutinku“ dings,
  visi jus sekantys nebesekys. Jo adresas daugiau niekada nebus naudojamas."). Switching off alone is also
  `DELETE /me/profile/public`, open behind the terms (8.22.2, `TermsExemptions`) and to a restricted member. Deleting the
  account and taking over an MP's profile switch it off too.
- **What the site cannot take back.** A retired address answers **410 Gone** (not 404): it tells search engines and
  archives that the page was removed on purpose and is not coming back, which makes them drop it sooner than an
  ordinary "not found"; an id never issued answers 404. The site stops serving the page at once, but a search engine's
  copy or snippet disappears only when that engine reads the address again, which the site does not control. The form
  says so (above), and so does the privacy notice.
- **"Apie mane" is part of the public profile and shows only with it:** one switch and one explanation, not two. The text
  can be written while the profile is off and is shown nowhere then.

## 8.25.2 The address and the page

- **`/nariai/{name-slug}-{id}`**, for example `/nariai/jonas-petraitis-7k3qm2x9ab`. The id (ten random lower-case letters
  and digits from an alphabet without look-alikes, `ProfileRules.NewId`, never the name) decides which profile it is: no
  squatting on names, no impersonation by address, two members with one name never share an address, and a changed
  display name keeps the page. The name part is made from the current display name as MPs' `/p/{slug}` are (one function,
  `AddressSlugs.Of`: Lithuanian letters to plain Latin, lower case, other characters a hyphen); a name with nothing to
  make it of gives the id alone. Any other name part (a changed name, a typo, the id alone) answers **301** to the
  current address, the query kept (`MemberAddresses`, before the page); so does the same address in other letter case or
  with a trailing slash (routing would answer them). A retired id still answers 410 and an unknown one 404, whatever the
  name part. A display name that ends in something shaped like an id makes an address that may read like another
  member's; the last part decides, so it leads only to its own profile. Every link to a profile is made by
  `ProfileRoutes.Page` (in emails `MemberAddress.Page`), and the profile's answers carry it (`address`). There is no
  sitemap and no canonical link on the site to update.
- **Indexed.** No `noindex` and no `X-Robots-Tag` on these pages: being found by name is part of what the person chose.
  (The trial deployment's own `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` for the whole site, 9.2, applies there.) The link preview
  (8.22.5) carries the name and one generic line, never "Apie mane".
- **What the page shows** (`MemberPage`): the label "Lietuva 2.0 narys"; the display name; under it "Sekti" and the number
  of followers in one line (8.25.5; a visitor gets the sign-in link); the party, only as 8.25.9 says; "Apie mane"; "Pranešti
  apie profilį"; and, under "Argumentai ir komentarai", the member's published arguments and the comments they wrote in
  their own voice that the bill's page shows, on bills whose record is not hidden, newest first, each with its bill
  (number and title), for an argument the side it was written for ("argumentas už", "argumentas prieš"), for a comment
  what it stands under ("komentaras po argumentu", "… po organizacijos pozicija", "… po Seimo nario atsakymu"), the date,
  the first part of the text and a link that lands on it on the bill's page (8.23.6). 10, then "Rodyti daugiau" adding 50
  (`ProfileLimits`, at most 500) by the page's address (`?rodyti=`), without script (8.16.4). Beside it: "Šį puslapį narys
  įsijungė pats. Čia matyti tik tai, ką jis paskelbė viešai."
- **What it never shows:** the person's own sides; "Man svarbu"; the "Sutinku" they gave (each is visible beside its
  comment, but a list of everything a person agreed with is a profile of opinions nobody asked to publish as a list); what
  they follow; party support, unless the person ticks it to be shown (8.25.9); invitations; organisations they
  represent; anything written as an organisation or an MP; hidden, waiting or withdrawn texts, and texts on a bill whose
  record is hidden; any count or statistic about the person's texts ("12 argumentų, 9 už"); any ranking; who follows them.
- **On a sheet** (8.16): the name, the number and "Apie mane" on one, the list on another.
- **Served fresh.** The page is server-rendered and readable without script, `Cache-Control: no-cache` for a visitor and
  `no-store` for a signed-in session, as every page answered to one (6.8), so a switched-off profile is never served from
  a cache. A retired id answers 410 with a title and one line ("Šio profilio nebėra"; "Narys išjungė savo viešą profilį
  arba ištrynė paskyrą. Šis adresas daugiau nenaudojamas."), an id never issued the site's 404 page; both carry
  `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` (there is nothing to index), a public page none. "Sekti" and "Pranešti" are islands; on the
  member's own page they show nothing to follow or report, and "Sekti" gives way to a link to "Profilis".

## 8.25.3 "Apie mane"

- **One text,** plain, up to `Texts:MaxLength` (8.23.3; `about.too-long`), written and changed in "Profilis", removable at
  any time. Paragraphs and lists as in every typed text (8.2, "A text's layout").
- **Links are allowed** (a LinkedIn or other social page, one's own site), rendered as in comments (8.23.3): `https://`
  only, `rel="nofollow ugc noopener noreferrer"`, a new tab, nothing fetched, no card.
- **The person's own contacts are allowed:** pre-screening does not refuse an email address or a phone number here;
  someone else's contact data is left to reports (ground "personal data") and a moderator's hide. **A personal code is
  refused** (`about.personal-code`): nobody needs to publish one, and it is the one identifier that is never safe for
  the person to give out (with a name it is enough to pose as the person in many official and commercial dealings, and it
  cannot be changed). Checked where it is written (`ProfileRules.CheckAbout`). With the page indexed, a published
  address or phone number also reaches search engines; the field's hint says so ("Rodoma tik viešame profilyje. Galima
  nuoroda (https://) ir jūsų pačių kontaktai – jie bus vieši ir juos ras paieškos sistemos. Asmens kodo rašyti
  negalima.").
- **A writing restriction** (8.3) blocks writing or changing it (`writing.restricted`): the text is for others to read.
  An existing text stays. Clearing it and switching the profile off stay open: removal is erasure, not writing.

## 8.25.4 The name beside a text

- **For a member with a public profile** the name beside their arguments, their comments in their own voice and their
  "Sutinku" is a link to the page (`data-card`). On pointer hover or keyboard focus a small card shows the name, the first
  part of "Apie mane" (about 200 characters) and a link "Profilis": the hint pattern of 8.15 (`js/lt2-cards.js`,
  listeners on the document as `lt2-hints.js`, placed beside the name, closed by Escape). Without script the link alone
  works, and on touch a press goes to the page. The card's text is a `<template>` per profile id: in the arguments' sheet
  once per page (`BillRecordResponse.Cards`), and in each comment thread once per person in the thread
  (`CommentThreadResponse.Cards`; the template's id carries the thread, so two threads on one page never share an id).
  The card carries no follower number.
- **For a member without one** the name is plain text, and nothing beside it leads anywhere.
- **The initials badge** (`MemberBadge`, owner 2026-11-19): beside the name that leads to the page, and beside the heading
  of the page itself, a small round badge with the person's initials: the first letter of the first and of the last word of
  the display name, upper case (Lithuanian letters as they are, "Rūta Mockutė" is "RM"; one word, one letter; a name with
  no letter, no badge). Its colour is one of eight neutral pairs of background and letters (`--badge-0…7` in `lt2.css`,
  light and dark, letters at least 4.5:1) chosen by a fixed hash of the profile's public id, so a person keeps one colour
  (`MemberBadges`). Server-rendered, no image and no script, hidden from assistive technology (the name beside it is the
  text), sized by the text around it. Beside an argument, a comment in the person's own voice, a name in "Sutinka" and a
  name in the follows list; never beside a member without a public profile, an organisation or an MP (an MP's page has its
  own initials, 7.3), and not in the card or the moderators' queue. **Members have no uploaded picture:** a face beside
  political texts, the moderation load, the storage and parsing risk and the drift towards a social network are not
  worth it.
- Never beside a statement, a response or a comment written as an organisation or an MP (8.23.2), which name no person;
  never in summaries (which carry no names, 8.4) or emails; the moderators' queue keeps the plain name.

## 8.25.5 Following a member

- **`follow` has the kind `member`** (5.4; the other kinds are `bill`, `mp` and `organisation`), keyed by the profile id.
  Any member with a public profile can be followed (`PUT|DELETE /api/v1/members/{id}/follow`, the participation limit); a
  follow of anyone else is refused with the same answer as an unknown id (`member.not-found`), and "Sekti" is shown only
  on a public profile.
- **Who follows is private, as every follow (8.11.2):** shown to nobody, the followed person and staff included; in the
  follower's export; deleted with either account. Ended at once when the person switches the profile off; the follower
  sees the member gone from their list, without a reason.
- **The number of followers is shown.** On the profile page, one plain line, "Sekėjai: 12"; the person sees the same
  number everyone does and nothing more. The rule for all four kinds is in 8.11.2: exact from 1 (the party group rule
  does not reach it, and for a member the more so, since the person chose to be followed), nothing with none, on the page
  only and nothing sorted or ranked by it. For a member "on the page only" also means not in the card and not beside the
  person's texts, which would put a popularity number next to each argument, the second number beside what people wrote
  that 8.14.3 refuses for statements.
- **What it sends,** by the follower's rhythm (8.24) and its own switch on the follows screen, "Kai sekamas narys
  paskelbia argumentą ar komentarą" (`notice_setting.members`): that the followed member published an argument or a
  comment in their own voice, under the member's display name, with the bill's number and title, the link to the text
  (8.23.6: `?argumentas=…#reason-{id}` or `?komentaras=…#comment-{id}`) and to the member's page, never the text, a
  side or a count. An argument that waited for a moderator counts when it is published. Recorded once per text in
  `notify.bill_event` (kind `member`, key the argument's or the comment's id, under its bill; no person in it;
  `MemberNotices` in Notifications, its own part of the gathered email; the payload says which, `what`); told by the
  follow job to those who followed before it was published and before the job saw it, never to its author, nothing
  older than three days, and nothing when by the time the email is made the text is no longer shown or the profile is no
  longer public.
- **Risks, and what answers them.**
  - **One person watched and pressed.** The profile switch is theirs and ends every follow at once. Blocking one
    particular follower is impossible without showing followers, which the design refuses. Harassment in comments has
    its answers: the report grounds, hiding, the writing restriction (8.3).
  - **The product drifting from "people tell MPs" towards a social network,** with a follower number on every thing that
    can be followed (8.11.2). What stands against it: nothing is ranked, sorted or listed by followers, the number stands
    only on the thing's own page, and who follows is invisible to everyone but the follower.

## 8.25.6 Moderation, data, API

- **Reports:** "Pranešti apie profilį" on the profile page, target the profile (its id; with the profile public, no other
  id is needed), grounds the general ones and **"Apsimeta kitu asmeniu ar pareigūnu"** first; one open report per
  reporter and profile; reporters are shown to nobody. A moderator hides "Apie mane" with a statement emailed to the
  person; it is then shown nowhere until the person writes another (screened and shown at once, and may be reported
  again), and "Profilis" tells the person it is hidden and why; on the list of hidden items with "Atkurti" (a statement)
  while not replaced. "Palikti" closes the reports with an optional note. Every member whose report was open is told the
  decision by email (`email.report-decided`, 8.3). The texts on the page are moderated where they stand (8.3, 8.23.8).
  Audited as `moderation.profile.hidden|kept|restored`, without the text. An administrator's menu on a profile there also holds
  "Užrakinti autoriaus paskyrą" (the full lock, 8.3), by the profile's moderation id, never an address.
- **Not a claim to office.** The text may not be the only claim to be an MP or a public official: an MP speaks on the site
  only through the take-over link (8.5), and a profile saying "Seimo narys" on an account that is not linked is
  impersonation, hidden on report. The moderation rules say so.
- **Data:** `identity.profile` has `about`, `about_changed_at`, `about_hidden_at`, `about_hidden_statement`,
  `public_profile_id` (null while off) and `public_since`; `identity.retired_profile_id` (`id`, `retired_at`) keeps
  retired ids with no account, so none is reused and each answers 410; `identity.profile_report` (target account, ground,
  optional text, reporter, open or closed), kept by Identity, which owns the profile; the consent `public-profile` in
  `identity.consent`; `notify.follow` kind `member`; `notify.notice_setting.members`; `appeals.reason.first_public_at`
  (when an argument first became public, 5.3), which the follow line and the page read. The follower number is counted
  from `notify.follow` when the page is made (the Notifications module gives it through the shared kernel, a number
  only). All in `identity` and `notify`, withheld from `lt2_readonly` as those schemas are. In the export (the text,
  whether the profile is public and since when, the consent, the reports the person made, the follows the person made;
  not who follows them); deleted with the account. The page is composed by the host from Identity (name, text, profile
  id), Notifications (the number) and Appeals (the texts, `IMemberTexts`), as the agenda is.
- **Audited:** `profile.public-on`, `profile.public-off`, `profile.about-changed`, never the text.
- **API:**

```
GET    /api/v1/me/profile                      the person's own state: "Apie mane", whether a moderator hid it and why,
                                               the profile id and address while public, the consent text's version in force
PUT    /api/v1/me/profile                      about, public, and to switch on consentVersion (WriteReason limit, 6.4)
DELETE /api/v1/me/profile/public               "Viešas profilis" off and nothing else (open behind the terms)
GET    /api/v1/members/{id}                    name, "Apie mane", the follower number, the party if shown, address
                                               (public, no-cache; 410 when retired, 404 when unknown)
GET    /api/v1/members/{id}/texts?shown=       published arguments and comments in their own voice, newest first
PUT    /api/v1/members/{id}/follow             DELETE /api/v1/members/{id}/follow
POST   /api/v1/members/{id}/report             ground, optional text (the report limit)
GET    /api/v1/moderation/profiles             reported profiles and hidden texts (the text and the reports, never an
                                               address, a side or a follow; ProfileModerationView in the queue's tabs
                                               profiliai and paslepti)
POST   /api/v1/moderation/profiles/{id}/hide   …/restore (statement, emailed)      …/keep (optional note)
```

  Public answers about arguments, comments and "Sutinku" carry, beside a name, the profile id of a member with a public
  profile (`ReasonResponse.AuthorProfileId`, `CommentAuthorResponse.ProfileId`, `CommentAgreerResponse.ProfileId`) and
  nothing for anyone else.
- **Terms and privacy:** both name the public profile, "Apie mane", being followed and the shown follower number as what
  the member chooses with one switch, and that search engines may keep a copy after it is switched off; the
  methodology's "Kas matoma viešai, o kas ne" names them too. A text can state a political opinion or a membership
  ("Partijos X narys"), and a profile gathers opinions the person published.

## 8.25.9 The party shown publicly

A member's party support is `identity.party_support` (5.2): one party per person from the parties that nominated sitting
MPs, set on "Profilis" with its own explicit consent (kind `party-support`, because a party preference is a political
opinion); private; read only through `IPartySupporters` for the summaries' group numbers of at least
`Digest:PartyGroupMinimum` (6.3, 8.4); the MP's own account declares none (8.5.4). On top of it a member may show that
party on their public profile:

- **Which party:** the one the member already marked as supported, not a free field. One fact, one place: a free field
  would let a person publish one party and support another, and it is what "Apie mane" is already for ("Partijos X narys"
  is allowed there, 8.25.6).
- **Off unless switched on, with its own consent:** a box "Rodyti viešame profilyje" under the party, offered only while
  the profile is public and a party is chosen; with the profile off the party's part says "Partiją rodyti viešame
  profilyje galima tik jį įjungus.". When the box is ticked, before saving, it shows the sentence the consent covers:
  "Partiją matys visi, kas atvers jūsų viešo profilio puslapį, ją gali rodyti ir paieškos sistemos; nuėmus žymę,
  pašalinus ar pakeitus partiją arba išjungus profilį, ji iš karto nebebus rodoma." Recorded as its own consent (kind
  `party-public`, versioned: `PartyPublicConsent.TextVersion`, `2026-11-17`, the form's `PartyPublicLimits.ConsentVersion`;
  another version is refused with `party.consent-outdated`). Never implied by "Viešas profilis": that switch covers the
  name, "Apie mane", the texts and being followed, not an opinion the person kept private. Shown only while both the
  profile is public and the consent is in force.
- **Gone at once:** unticking the box, removing the party, changing the party or switching the profile off withdraws
  `party-public` and the page stops showing it; switching the profile on again does not bring it back by itself. A
  changed party is shown only after the box is given again, since the consent covered the party it was given for (the
  form sends the box again when it is still ticked beside the new choice, so the person sees what is published).
- **The MP's own account** supports no party and shows none (`mp.takes-no-part`); its office members are ordinary members
  here.
- **The party group rule (`Digest:PartyGroupMinimum`, 8.4)** is unchanged by the party shown. That rule is about counts
  of a group's sides; one person showing their own party is their own publication, like an argument. The summaries'
  group numbers still count everyone with a party, shown or not.
- **What else can be inferred:** with the party shown on a person's page beside their arguments, a reader can set the
  person's stated party beside the sides their arguments were written for; nothing more than the person published. No
  count of people per party is shown anywhere (there is no list of profiles and nothing is listed by party).
- **Where it stands:** on the profile's own page only, one line under the name, "Palaikoma partija: {party}" (the name
  after a colon, never declined); never in the card beside a text (8.25.4), beside an argument or comment, in the texts'
  answer or in any list.
- **API:** `GET /api/v1/me/profile` carries `partyPublic` and `partyPublicConsentVersion`; `PUT /api/v1/me/profile` with
  `partyPublic` and `partyPublicConsentVersion` gives `party-public` (`party.consent-outdated`, `party.none`,
  `party.profile-off`, `mp.takes-no-part`, under the field `partyPublic`, 8.28.5); `DELETE /api/v1/me/party-support/public`
  withdraws it and is open behind the terms (8.22.2, `TermsExemptions`), as is `DELETE /api/v1/me/party-support`. `GET /api/v1/members/{id}` carries `party`, null unless
  shown. The page reads it through `IPublicProfiles.ShownPartyAsync`, which answers only for a public profile with the
  consent in force.
- **Data:** no new table: the consent row in `identity.consent` (kind `party-public`) is the box; withdrawn rows stay as
  the other consents do. In the export (`partySupport.shownOnPublicProfile` and the consent among the consents); gone
  with the account. Audited `party.public-on` and `party.public-off` with why (`box`, `party`, `profile`), never the
  party.
