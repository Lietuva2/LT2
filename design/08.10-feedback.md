# 8.10 Feedback from members

Members tell the team what is broken, unclear or missing, without leaving the page.

- **Entry.** A round speech-balloon button at the bottom corner of every page opens a panel with the form; the panel's
  heading ("Atsiliepimai Lietuva 2.0 komandai", with "Tai ne pokalbis: žinutę skaito komanda.") says what it is, so it
  does not look like live chat. It is a disclosure (`<details>`), so it opens and closes without JavaScript; it is
  labelled "Atsiliepimai" for screen readers and on hover, reachable by keyboard, and its slide respects reduced motion.
  The button floats in the bottom corner at every width, phones included; every page ends with room for it, so nothing
  at the end of a page lies under it (while scrolling it passes over the page, as any floating button does). The open
  panel lies over the page at a readable width (24rem; on a phone the full width less the margins), never wider or
  taller than the window, scrolling inside itself when the form is longer than the screen; covering the page while it
  is open is expected. Without JavaScript the button, which shows a close mark while open, closes it; with the script
  there is also a "Uždaryti" button in the panel and Escape, focus moves into the panel when the person opens it (the
  text field, or the heading for a visitor) and back to the button when it closes. After the panel's form is sent, the
  page comes back with the panel open on the thanks. A plain page `/atsiliepimai` has the same content, for linking; the
  footer links it.
- **A member's form**: one text up to 2 000 characters; an optional kind (klaida, pasiūlymas, neaišku, kita); the page
  they are on (path only) and the browser's name and version, both shown before sending and each removable; and a tick
  box "Galite man atsakyti". Ticked, the item is linked to the account; unticked, it is stored with no link to the
  account at all (a random id and only the day). A hint asks not to write which side one took on a bill. The form is
  server-rendered and posts back to the page, so it needs no WebAssembly and works without JavaScript. An account that
  has not accepted the terms of use (8.22.2) cannot send.
- **A visitor** sees no form: "Atsiliepimus svetainėje gali palikti prisijungę Lietuva 2.0 naudotojai." and "Nepavyksta
  prisijungti? Parašykite mums:" with the moderators' contact address; the same line is on the sign-in page.
- **GitHub issues**: the panels and `/atsiliepimai` link to the repository's issues behind "Pranešti viešai GitHub"
  (`Feedback:IssuesUrl`, an `https` address; nothing when unset), saying plainly that an issue there is public and
  appears under the person's GitHub name. A link only: no script from GitHub, no request to it, nothing forwarded.
- **Staff** (moderators and administrators, staff passkey) at `/moderavimas/atsiliepimai`: a list (newest first, at
  most 200) filtered by kind and status (new, seen, done), an internal note per item (up to 1 000 characters, for
  example the issue's address), and for linked items a reply sent through the outbox to the member's address, which
  staff never see, with the display name shown; a reply marks a new item seen. Unlinked items show no author and have
  no reply. Changes and replies are audited without the note's or the item's text. API: `POST /api/v1/feedback` (a
  member), `GET /api/v1/moderation/feedback`, `PUT …/{id}` and `POST …/{id}/reply` (staff).
- **Notice**: at most one email a day to the team address (`Feedback:TeamAddress`) with the number of new items and the
  staff page's address, never the text; nothing when there is nothing new or no address is set.
- **Rules**: ten items a day per account (counted in memory, so an unlinked item gets no link through the counter); an
  account restricted from writing (8.3) can still send feedback; kept for `Feedback:KeepMonths` months (12), then
  deleted by the daily clean-up; linked items are in the export and go with the account; never in logs; never combined
  with sides; not public and not moderated. It lives in the Identity module, beside the account it may be linked to, the
  address replies go to, the export, the deletion and the daily clean-up.
