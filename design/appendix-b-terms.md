# Appendix B. Terms

| English (this document) | Lithuanian | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| appeal | kreipimasis | What people on a bill ask of MPs; kept on the bill's record, one per bill, on the bill's page (8.12). |
| position | pozicija | A person's side on a bill: for or against. Private. |
| reason | argumentas | A short public text supporting one side. |
| response | atsakymas | An MP's stance and explanation, published once for everyone. |
| summary | suvestinė | The email to MPs and its public copy. |
| comment | komentaras | A remark under an argument, an organisation's statement or an MP's response, in "Aptarimas" (8.23). |
| initiator | iniciatorius | A member named beside a bill's description of source `initiator` (5.3); no rights. No code path makes one. |
| delegate | patikėtinis | Reserved for delegation, which is neither designed nor built. |
