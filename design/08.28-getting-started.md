# 8.28 Getting started: the menu's lines, the new member's start, "Kur ką rasti", the profile's one form

The Lithuanian texts are drafts for the native reader. The mock-ups are `design/ui/pradzia-naujas-narys.html` and
`design/ui/metodika-kur-ka-rasti.html`.

A person who has just registered knows what the visitor's "Kaip tai veikia" (8.7) told them and nothing about where
things are. The menu's words alone ("Projektai", "Suvestinės") do not say what is behind them, and the member's front
page greets them as someone coming back.

## 8.28.1 The approach

- **No guided tour.** No overlay that walks a person through the screen, no highlighted elements, no "next" buttons. A
  tour is pinned to where things stand on a screen and breaks whenever a page is rearranged; it needs script on public
  pages; and it needs a per-person mark that it was seen, which is either a new key in the browser (a design change,
  8.22.1) or a new field on the account.
- **The explanation stands where the thing is,** in three places: the menu says what each of its pages is (8.28.2); the
  front page tells a member who has not taken a side yet how to start (8.28.3); the methodology page lists where
  everything is (8.28.4).
- **Nothing is stored to show or hide it.** Whether the start is shown follows from what the account has done, which
  the site already knows. There is nothing to dismiss and nothing to remember.
- **Nothing is worded as new** (`AGENTS.md`): the texts say what a thing is, never that it was added.
- **It does not teach by email.** A welcome email would be a new kind of email (8.24) and is not part of this.

## 8.28.2 One description of the menu

- **One list describes every item of the menu and the footer:** its address, its name, one line that says what the
  page is, and where it stands (the menu, the "Projektai" dropdown, the account item, a footer group). The menu, the
  dropdown, the tab row under the pages' headings, the footer (its group "Naršyti", which repeats the menu, and its
  other groups, 8.21) and "Kur ką rasti" are all drawn from that list.
- **A test fails when an item has no line,** so an item cannot be added or renamed without its explanation. Moving,
  renaming or removing an item is one change in one place, and every place that shows it follows.
- **The menu's items do not change.** The dropdown of "Projektai" and the tab row hold the four tabs of 8.21 in their
  order: "Darbotvarkė", "Projektai", "Balsavimai", "Suvestinės". The tab row and the footer show names only.
- **Each entry of the dropdown shows its line under its name,** for everyone, signed in or not, with no script. The
  panel is about 21rem wide; under 32rem it is as wide as the page and does not hang from the item, so the lines have
  room on a phone. The current page's name is marked; the line is not underlined.
- **The items of the menu's one line ("Seimo nariai", "Statistika", the account item) show no line there:** the menu
  must stay on one line from 340 px (8.21). Their lines are read in "Kur ką rasti".
- **The lines.** One sentence each, saying what the page holds, not how to use it.

  | Item | Line |
  |---|---|

  | Balsavimai | Kaip Seimas balsavo: visi balsavimai nuo naujausio. |
  | Suvestinės | Ką apie dalyvių prašymus sužino Seimo nariai. |
  | Seimo nariai | Kiekvieno Seimo nario pareigos, balsavimai ir atsakymai. |

  | Paskyra | Jūsų profilis, sekami projektai, laiškai ir duomenys. |
  | Metodika | Iš kur duomenys, kaip skaičiuojama ir kas matoma viešai. |
  | Moderavimo taisyklės | Kas skelbiama, kas slepiama ir kaip tam paprieštarauti. |

## 8.28.3 The new member's start on the front page

- **Who sees it:** a member who has never taken a side. The account's own answer for the front page says so: it has
  not given the positions consent, which is asked before the first side (6.5, `GET /me/bills`). No new field, no new
  request. A visitor does not see it (the visitor has "Kaip tai veikia", 8.7), nor does an MP's own account, which
  takes no side (8.5.4).
- **It goes away by itself** when the member takes a first side, in a row or on a bill's page, without a reload. It
  has no button to close it. A member who withdraws the positions consent has no sides left (6.5) and sees the start
  again.
- **The greeting.** For this member the page's title reads "Sveiki prisijungę"; everyone else's reads "Sveiki
  sugrįžę" (8.7). The served title is "Sveiki" for every member; the browser completes it once the member's own answer
  is read, so nobody sees one greeting replaced by the other. Before the island has started, and with JavaScript off,
  the title is "Sveiki".
- **Four steps:** choose a bill; say what you ask; explain why; MPs learn of it. The fourth is the purpose of the
  other three.
- **The full steps on a wide screen, the short ones on a phone.** The full form is the visitor's numbered steps (a
  title and a sentence or two each, side by side); the short form is one line a step, so the agenda's heading stays on
  a phone's first screen. The short form is shown under 54rem, where the agenda's rows become one column (8.7); the
  full form from there up. Both are in the page and the width chooses, with no script.
- **No sentence on what is private:** the consent before the first side, the methodology page and the visitor's
  introduction say that a person's side is shown to nobody.
- **The texts.** Under the title "Pradėkite nuo vieno projekto":

  | Step | Full: title | Full: text | Short |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | Pasirinkite projektą | Žemiau – ką Seimas planuoja svarstyti artimiausiomis dienomis. Pavadinimas veda į projekto puslapį. | Pasirinkite projektą žemiau. |
  | 2 | Nurodykite, ko prašote | Prie „Prašau balsuoti“ paspauskite „už“ arba „prieš“. Paspaudę dar kartą, atsiimsite. | Paspauskite „už“ arba „prieš“; paspaudę dar kartą, atsiimsite. |
  | 3 | Paaiškinkite kodėl | Projekto puslapyje parašykite argumentą arba pažymėkite „Man svarbu“ prie kitų. | Projekto puslapyje paaiškinkite kodėl. |
  | 4 | Seimo nariai sužino | Kai dalyvių skaičius pasiekia ribą, projektas patenka į suvestinę Seimo nariams: abi pusės ir svarbiausi argumentai. | Pasiekus ribą, projektas patenka į suvestinę Seimo nariams. |

  Under the steps, in both forms, the link "Kaip tai veikia →" to `/metodika#kaip-tai-veikia`.
  - Step 2 is where a person learns that pressing the pressed side again takes it back; no line under the side buttons
    says it (8.7, 8.12.1).
  - Step 4's full text is the fourth step of "Kaip tai veikia" (8.7), the same text, not a copy. While nothing is sent
    it ends with the same sentence as there, "Kol vyksta bandymas, suvestinės sudaromos, bet Seimo nariams
    nesiunčiamos." (8.15: that nothing is sent stays inline). The short step 4 does not repeat it: the member's front
    page carries the note about the trial at its foot (8.7).
  - The names of the controls in the texts ("Prašau balsuoti", "už", "prieš", "Man svarbu") are taken from the
    controls' own texts, so a renamed button is renamed here.
- **Where it stands:** on the ground between the greeting and the agenda, like the visitor's introduction (the same
  exception of 8.16). The exception of 8.15 for the visitor's introduction covers it too: a person who has not taken
  part yet has to learn what to do.
- **"Jūsų projektai" steps aside:** while the start is shown and the member follows nothing on the agenda, the panel
  is left out; its one line ("Šiuo metu darbotvarkėje nėra projektų, kuriuos sekate.") says nothing to a person who has
  just come. A new member who already follows a bill on the agenda sees the start and then the panel.
- **Privacy (8.7).** The page the server renders stays the same for every member. The start and the greeting's second
  word are filled in the browser from the member's own answer and are never rendered on the server or cached.

## 8.28.4 "Kur ką rasti" on the methodology page

- Under the four steps at the top of `/metodika`, a part "Kur ką rasti" (`/metodika#kur-ka-rasti`): the items of the
  menu, then the footer's own ("Apie", "Dalyvauti"), each with its line, each name a link. The four entries of
  "Projektai" stand under it, indented.
- It is drawn from the list of 8.28.2, so it cannot differ from the menu. It is the one place where the lines of
  "Seimo nariai", "Statistika" and the account item are read.
- The start's link "Kaip tai veikia →" lands just above it.

## 8.28.5 From the registration to the start: the profile's one form

- **A registration just completed lands on "Profilis"** (8.11), to fill it out; one begun from a bill's page ends on
  that bill.
- **"Profilis" is one form with one button.** The display name, "Apie mane", the box "Viešas profilis", party support
  and its box "Rodyti viešame profilyje" (8.21, 8.25) are one form with one "Išsaugoti" at its foot, not three forms
  with a button each.
  - Each consent stays a box of its own with its own text, unticked until the person ticks it ("Viešas profilis", party
    support, the party on the public profile). The button saves what is filled and ticked and never stands for a
    consent.
- **One request to one endpoint.** `PUT /api/v1/me/profile` carries the whole form: the display name, "Apie mane",
  whether the profile is public, the party supported, whether the party shows on the public profile, and the version
  of each consent text the person saw. `GET /api/v1/me/profile` answers the whole form's state. The separate writes go:
  `PUT /me/display-name`, `PUT /me/party-support` and `PUT /me/party-support/public`.
  - **Saved whole or not at all.** Every part is checked before anything is written. If any part is refused (a name
    that is taken, a text over its length, a consent text that is out of date, a writing restriction), nothing is
    saved: the answer names every refused field, the form stays open with what the person typed, and each such field
    says why.
  - A part that did not change is not a change: it is not checked as a write, refused or audited.
  - What is written is written together, in one transaction: the values, each consent given or withdrawn with its
    text version, and the audit entries each part has today.
  - **The three withdrawals stay as requests of their own:** `DELETE /me/profile/public`, `DELETE /me/party-support`
    and `DELETE /me/party-support/public`. An account that has not accepted the terms can withdraw what it consented
    to but cannot save the form (8.22.2). The form itself withdraws by being saved with the box unticked.
- **On the visit that follows the registration the button leads on.** There it reads "Išsaugoti ir tęsti", saves, and
  opens the front page, also when nothing was filled, so nobody has to fill the profile to go on. On every later visit
  it reads "Išsaugoti" and the person stays on the screen.
  - The registration's landing address carries a mark after `#` that says this is that visit; nothing is stored.
  - **The notice:** the front page shows "Profilis išsaugotas." once, above the start, as a status message. It too is
    carried by a mark after `#` in the address, taken out of the address once the notice is shown; nothing is stored in
    the browser or on the account (8.22.1).
  - When the save is refused nothing leads on: the form stays open, as on any visit.

## 8.28.6 What does not change

- No cookie, no storage key (the inventory of 8.22.1 stays as it is), no new field on the account.
- The visitor's front page and "Kaip tai veikia" (8.7).
- What a member who has taken a side sees on the front page.
- The hints beside terms and numbers (8.15) stay the place for "Kas yra riba?" and the like; the start does not
  explain terms.

## 8.28.7 Done when

- Every item of the menu and the footer comes from one list, and a test fails for an item without a line.
- The dropdown of "Projektai" shows its four entries with their lines, opens without script, and fits at 340, 360, 390
  and 1280 px with no sideways scroll; the menu is still one line at those widths.
- A member who has not given the positions consent sees "Sveiki prisijungę" and the four steps: the full form at
  1280 px, the short form at 390 px, where the agenda's heading is on the first screen (390 × 844).
- After that member takes a side in a row, the start is gone and the title reads "Sveiki sugrįžę", without a reload.
- A member who has taken a side, a visitor and an MP's own account never see the start.
- The page's HTML is identical for a member who sees the start and one who does not.
- While nothing is sent, step 4's full text says so; when sending is on, it does not.
- The storage test (8.22.1) passes unchanged.
- "Kur ką rasti" lists exactly the items of the menu and the footer, and each of its links leads to its page.
- "Profilis" has one "Išsaugoti"; a valid form is saved by one `PUT /me/profile`; a form with one refused field saves
  nothing, names the field and keeps what was typed; the three earlier writes are gone and the three withdrawals work
  for an account that has not accepted the terms.
- A registration just completed lands on "Profilis" with "Išsaugoti ir tęsti", which opens the front page showing
  "Profilis išsaugotas." once; a reload of that page does not show it again; a later visit to "Profilis" has
  "Išsaugoti" and stays.
